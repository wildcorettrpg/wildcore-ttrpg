## **1. Dual-License Structure**
The WildCore ruleset (“the Work”) is available under two parallel licenses:

- **WildCore Open License (WOL):** Free, open, share-alike, community-driven.  
- **WildCore Dual-License (WOL-DL):** Commercial, negotiated, and limited-scope.

Creators may choose either license depending on their project’s needs.

## **2. Purpose**
The WOL-DL exists to support:
- Official WildCore products and expansions  
- Commercial partnerships and co-developed works  
- Closed-source or proprietary digital tools  
- Licensed adaptations for other media (video games, novels, etc.)  
- Corporate or institutional use requiring custom terms

All community content remains under the WOL.  
All official or proprietary content may use the WOL-DL by agreement.
