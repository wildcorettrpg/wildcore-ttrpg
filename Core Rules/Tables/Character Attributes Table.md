# Character Attributes Table

| Attribute  | Description                                                                                                                                                          |
| :--------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fortitude  | Physical resistance to poison, disease, exhaustion, and environmental hazards (extreme temperatures, toxic atmosphere, oxygen deprivation). Not used for Consciousness Checks. |
| Reflex     | Used when a character attempts to dodge, parry, or block. Provides a flat bonus on these defensive rolls.                              |
| Social     | Determines a character's capacity for social influence — persuasion, intimidation, negotiation, and contests of will or empathy with others. Built via the [Social Skill](Social%20Skill.md) attribute skill tree. |
| Strength   | Used for melee and ranged attacks and opposed physical checks (grapples, shoves, etc.). Grants 1 bonus damage die per 3 points on physical attacks.                |
| Willpower  | Used for magical attacks and resistance to domination, intimidation, and similar Social-based effects. Grants 1 bonus damage die per 3 points on spell attacks. Used for Consciousness Checks — when a character's Health drops to 7 or below, they roll their dice pool + Willpower. |
| Health (Max / Current) | Not a core attribute — a two-value tracked stat. **Maximum Health** begins at 25 for all characters; increased through the [Health Skill](../Skills/Universal/Attributes/Health%20Skill.md) tree, Fortitude VII and IX (+10 each), and lineage abilities. **Current Health** represents the character's present condition; reduced by damage, restored by healing. Cannot exceed Maximum Health or drop below 0. |
