## Size

WildCore uses six size categories, adopted from the d20 standard. Size affects melee combat when there is a significant difference between attacker and defender — specifically, whether an attack Opposition Check is required at all, and whether the smaller combatant has an easier time avoiding the larger one.

### Size Categories

|Size|Examples|
|---|---|
|Tiny|Small animals, sprites|
|Small|Dwarf, Kin, Kith; some Shapeshifter animal forms|
|Medium|Human, Elf, Faerie, Shapeshifter (humanoid form); some Shapeshifter animal forms|
|Large|Large creatures and monsters; some Shapeshifter animal forms|
|Huge|Giants, large dragons, great beasts|
|Gargantuan|Ancient dragons, siege creatures, legendary monsters|

For Shapeshifter animal form sizes, see Shapeshifter.

### Size and Melee Combat

#### Auto-Hit (Attacking Up)

When a combatant attacks a target **two or more size categories larger** than themselves with a melee weapon, no attack Opposition Check is required — the attack automatically hits. A Kith (Small) attacking a horse (Large) cannot miss at melee range. The attack still rolls a damage pool normally.

|Attacker Size|Auto-hits targets of size…|
|---|---|
|Tiny|Medium and above|
|Small|Large and above|
|Medium|Huge and above|
|Large|Gargantuan|

One category difference (e.g., Small vs. Medium) still requires a normal attack Opposition Check.

#### Dodge Bonus (Defending Down)

When a combatant defends against a melee attack from a target **two or more size categories larger** than themselves, they gain **+2 to their Dodge pool** against that attack. A larger attacker has a harder time landing a precise blow on a significantly smaller, faster target.

|Defender Size|+2 Dodge vs. attackers of size…|
|---|---|
|Tiny|Medium and above|
|Small|Large and above|
|Medium|Huge and above|
|Large|Gargantuan|

This bonus applies to the Dodge pool only. Parry and Block are unaffected by size.

### Ranged and Spellcasting Exceptions

These rules apply to **melee attacks only**. Ranged attacks and spellcasting are unaffected by size — a Kith archer still makes a normal attack Opposition Check against a hill giant, and a giant's thrown boulder still requires a normal attack roll against a Kith.
