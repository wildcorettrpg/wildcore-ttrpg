# Weapons

## Purpose

1. This table defines the base damage die for every weapon category referenced by Melee Attack and Ranged Attack.
2. A weapon's base damage die is the die rolled before any attribute or skill damage bonuses are applied (see [Damage](../Encounters/Damage.md) §2-3).
3. Bonus damage dice from attributes and skills use this same die (per [Damage](../Encounters/Damage.md) §3.4).
4. Each weapon also lists a **Size** (Tiny/Small/Medium/Large, per [Inventory](Inventory.md) §2) for inventory purposes and a **Cost** in gold pieces (gp) for purchase. Costs are ported from d20 source material — see [Currency](Currency.md) for the currency note. Cost has no effect on inventory slots or carrying capacity.

## Unarmed

1. Unarmed attacks have no base damage die — damage is Strength bonus only (minimum 1).
2. Unarmed damage equals the attacker's flat Strength bonus, minimum 1 point.
3. Unarmed attacks have no Size or Cost — they require no equipment.

## Ammunition

1. Ranged weapons that require ammunition (bows, crossbows, slings) consume ammunition appropriate to the weapon. See the tables below for types and costs.
2. Each bundle of 20 pieces of ammunition is Medium and occupies one inventory slot. Bundles do not stack.
3. Thrown weapons (dart, javelin, thrown handaxe, thrown knife, thrown light hammer, thrown spear, thrown trident) are their own ammunition — each throw uses the weapon itself.

## Access and Use

1. A character may use any weapon — there are no class or background restrictions on weapon access.
2. A weapon's category determines its base damage die and which Melee Attack or Ranged Attack tier is required to use it without penalty (light: tier I, medium: tier IV, heavy: tier VI).
3. The Narrator may introduce setting-specific weapons; assign them to the closest matching category, size, and cost above.

## Notes for Playtesting

Damage dice are Velocity-native values derived from d20 SRD base damage stepped up one die tier (d4→d6, d6→d8, d8→d10, d10→d12, d12/2d6→3d6). Weapon categories (Light/Medium/Heavy) reflect proficiency access tiers, not damage tiers — category and damage die are independent. Costs are ported from d20 source material and are expected to be tuned once tested against Velocity's damage, mitigation, and Health scale.
