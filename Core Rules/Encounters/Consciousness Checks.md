# Consciousness Checks

## When a Consciousness Check Occurs

A Consciousness Check is required **any time a character loses Health while at or below 7** — including the hit that first drops them to 7 or below, and every subsequent source of Health loss while they remain there. The threshold is fixed for all characters regardless of Maximum Health.

This means a character Bleeding Out at 5 Health makes a new Consciousness Check at the end of every round when the Bleeding Out tick fires. Each loss event is a separate check.

A character who is already Unconscious does not make further Consciousness Checks.

## Making a Consciousness Check

A Consciousness Check is a special check — not a standard Opposition Check. The character rolls their [dice pool](Dice%20Pools.md) + **Willpower**, adding any dice or flat bonuses from applicable skills.

The result is compared to the **damage or Health loss value** that triggered the check:

- **Success (meets or exceeds):** The character remains conscious.
- **Failure (falls short):** The character becomes **Unconscious**.

## While Unconscious

An Unconscious character cannot act, cannot take Reactions, and is automatically **Vulnerable** to attacks — no defensive roll is made. They are also **Prone** and cannot move. If they are Bleeding Out, they continue to lose Health each round.

An Unconscious character remains unconscious until the Unconscious condition is separately removed — see [Stabilization](../Characters/Stabilization.md) §Forced Awakening.

## Skill Bonuses

Some skills add to Consciousness Check rolls:

- **Spellcasting V** grants +1d10 to Consciousness Checks. A caster at this depth of investment has conditioned their body to fight through damage that would knock an untrained person unconscious — this is learned resilience, not a supernatural effect.

Additional dice or bonuses may be granted by lineage abilities or other skills where their descriptions explicitly apply.

## Relationship to Bleeding Out

Bleeding Out ticks at the end of a character's turn. Each tick is a Health loss event — if the character is at or below 7 Health when the tick fires, it triggers a Consciousness Check. The loss value for the check is the amount lost that tick (1 per active Bleeding Out stack). A character Bleeding Out at low Health may face a check every round until stabilized.

See [Dying](../Characters/Dying.md) for Bleeding Out rules and [Stabilization](../Characters/Stabilization.md) for how to end it.
