# Consciousness Checks

## When a Consciousness Check Occurs

A Consciousness Check is required **any time a character loses Health while at or below 7** — including the hit that first drops them to 7 or below, and every subsequent source of Health loss while they remain there. The threshold is fixed for all characters regardless of Maximum Health.

This means a character [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out) at 5 Health makes a new Consciousness Check at the end of every round when the [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out) tick fires. Each loss event is a separate check.

A character who is already [Unconscious](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious) does not make further Consciousness Checks.

## Making a Consciousness Check

A Consciousness Check is a special check — not a standard Opposition Check. The character rolls their [dice pool](../Core%20Mechanics/Dice%20Pools.md) + **Willpower**, adding any dice or flat bonuses from applicable skills.

The result is compared to the **damage or Health loss value** that triggered the check:

- **Success (meets or exceeds):** The character remains conscious.
- **Failure (falls short):** The character becomes **[Unconscious](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious)**.

## While [Unconscious](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious)

An [Unconscious](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious) character cannot act, cannot take Reactions, and is automatically **[Vulnerable](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#vulnerable)** to attacks — no defensive roll is made. They are also **[Prone](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#prone)** and cannot move. If they are [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out), they continue to lose Health each round.

An [Unconscious](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious) character remains unconscious until the [Unconscious](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious) condition is separately removed — see [Stabilization](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Stabilization.md) §Forced Awakening.

## Skill Bonuses

Some skills add to Consciousness Check rolls:

- **Spellcasting V** grants +1d10 to Consciousness Checks. A caster at this depth of investment has conditioned their body to fight through damage that would knock an untrained person unconscious — this is learned resilience, not a supernatural effect.

Additional dice or bonuses may be granted by race abilities or other skills where their descriptions explicitly apply.

## Relationship to [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out)

[Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out) ticks at the end of a character's turn. Each tick is a Health loss event — if the character is at or below 7 Health when the tick fires, it triggers a Consciousness Check. The loss value for the check is the amount lost that tick (1 per active [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out) stack). A character [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out) at low Health may face a check every round until stabilized.

See [Dying](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Dying.md) for [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out) rules and [Stabilization](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Stabilization.md) for how to end it.
