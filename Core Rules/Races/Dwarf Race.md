# Dwarf Race

## Origin

Dwarves were Ordred's idea. Ordred — Anyar of Earth, the Builder, the Architect, the Laborer — brought his vision to Balathor and asked him to make it real. Balathor obliged, as he always does, and the result was a people made in Ordred's image: compact, powerful, precise, and constitutionally incapable of looking at a structure without imagining how to improve it. They build soaring cities of gleaming crystal and throw skybridges across open sky hundreds of feet up with the same casual confidence they bring to everything else. They work harder than anyone and celebrate louder than anyone. They know no fear, which is occasionally a problem.

That same fearlessness has its limits. Enclosed natural spaces — caves, dense forests, anywhere the sky disappears — produce in Dwarves a disorientation that has nothing to do with cowardice and everything to do with the absence of horizon. Ships are worse still. A Dwarf aboard a seagoing vessel is a sorry sight: the deck never stops moving, and a people built for precision on solid ground find nothing to grab onto, aside from the railing as he violently hurls his last meal into the relentless waves. Ordred never imagined seagoing vessels. You don't build cities on water. His people were not designed for them, and it shows.

**Size:** Small
**Base Movement Speed:** 30 ft
**Languages**: Common, Dwarven

## Traits

### Builder's Physique
Dwarves train from childhood hauling crystal and raising architectural marvels. +2 on Strength-based Opposition Checks.

### Dwarven Endurance
Dwarves work harder than anyone. +2 on Fortitude Opposition Checks against exhaustion, physical strain, or prolonged exertion.

### Fearless
Dwarves are immune to fear effects. Automatic success on Willpower Opposition Checks to resist fear.

### Head for Heights
Dwarves are wholly at home at elevation on solid ground. Automatic success on Willpower Opposition Checks against vertigo on land. +2 on Opposition Checks requiring physical precision at elevation.

### Low Center of Gravity
Dwarves' compact, powerful build makes them exceptionally difficult to move against their will. +2 on Opposition Checks to resist being tripped, knocked prone, or forced into movement.

### Structural Instinct
A lifetime spent constructing crystal cities and throwing skybridges across open sky gives Dwarves an innate understanding of built environments. +2 on Investigation Opposition Checks involving architecture, construction, structural integrity, or identifying weaknesses in built structures.

## Racial Flaws

### Arcane Disconnect
Ordred is the most material of all Anyar — Earth, stone, crystal, permanence. The Magesea barely flows through his creations. Dwarves have no natural affinity for magic and find it harder to learn than most. −2 on Spellcasting Opposition Checks. Spellcasting skills cost 1 additional Skill Point to purchase.

### Greenhorn
A Dwarf aboard a seagoing vessel is wholly out of their element. −4 on all Opposition Checks while aboard a seagoing vessel. Low Center of Gravity provides no benefit aboard seagoing vessels.

### Heavy Footed
The dense build that anchors a Dwarf against being tripped or thrown does not lend itself to quickness. −2 on Reflex-based Opposition Checks requiring speed, agility, or rapid reaction.

### Ordred's Weight
Dwarves are extraordinarily dense for their size — a consequence of the same build that makes them so difficult to move. They do not float. Automatic failure on Opposition Checks to swim or stay afloat.

### Thunderfoot
Crystal cities and skybridges are visible from anywhere in Velaria. Dwarves don't hide things — they build monuments. That's not just culture; it's physics. A dense, heavy frame moving on hard surfaces does not move quietly. −2 on Stealth Opposition Checks.
