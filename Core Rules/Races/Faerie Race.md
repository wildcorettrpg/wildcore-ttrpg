# Faerie Race

Faeries are beings of pure light wearing flesh like a garment — and once a day, they must take it off.

They are the descendants of The Fae, a being who has walked the world since its earliest age, and they carry that inheritance in everything they are. They cannot lie. This is not a discipline or a cultural value — it is constitutional, built into them by Luniel, who claimed them as her own and gave them truth as a birthright. They love the moon and the stars with the same unreasoning certainty that roots turn toward water. They are luminous even in their physical bodies: at night, a faint silver or golden glimmer surrounds them, and in darkness they can produce and direct their own light.

They are also grieving, though they do not always look it.

Thousands of years ago, a faction of Faeries learned to lie. Luniel destroyed them for it — stripped their magic, severed their connection to the Light, and twisted their bodies into the shriveled forms of Imps. Then she turned to the Faeries who had known and said nothing, and she took something from them too: the mate bond. The recognition, specific and irreversible, that a particular person was permanently and cosmically yours. It happened once per Faerie, if it happened at all, and it has not happened to anyone in thousands of years.

Faeries kill Imps on sight. The hatred is total and has the texture of grief.

They are the same height as humans, lighter of frame, and move with an almost ethereal quality even in their physical form. They float above the ground rather than walking on it — which is less a choice than a natural consequence of what they are — and difficult terrain does not slow them. In their Ethereal Form, which they must assume for four consecutive hours each day, they are insubstantial: unharmable by ordinary weapons, unable to physically interact with the world, and healing faster than they otherwise would. This is not a power. It is a need. Faeries who do not take their bodies off pay for it.

They are incapable of deception and can sense when others are being deceptive. In societies that know what Faeries are, their word is considered binding without further confirmation. In societies that don't, they tend to be regarded as either naive or profoundly unsettling, depending on context.

**Size:** Medium
**Base Movement Speed:** 35 ft
**Languages**: Common, Fae, Old Anaric

## Traits

### Ethereal Form
Once per day, a Faerie must assume their true form: a purely ethereal state with no physical body. They may move freely in this form, but cannot physically interact with the world. Normal weapons cannot pierce or harm them while ethereal; magical weapons, spells, and mind-affecting abilities such as charm and compulsion can. While ethereal, a Faerie recovers 2 Health per hour of rest (compared to the base rate of 1 Health per four hours), and doubles Health restored by potions and Healing skills. The Faerie must remain in Ethereal Form for four consecutive hours. A Faerie who does not assume Ethereal Form suffers −4 on all Opposition Checks the following day.

When in their ethereal form, a faerie gains +2 to Opposition Checks to resist magical effects.

### Incapable of Deception
Faeries cannot lie. This is not a cultural norm, a discipline, or a matter of will — it is what they are. A Faerie is constitutionally incapable of making a false statement. They can decline to speak, choose what to reveal, and frame truths carefully — but they cannot say something they know to be false.

In societies that know of them, Faeries are widely regarded as incapable of deception — their word is considered binding without oath or instrument to confirm it.

### Lightborne Step
Faeries walk above the ground, hovering just above its surface. They ignore movement penalties from [Difficult Terrain](../Encounters/Movement.md#difficult-terrain) and can pass over bodies of water, deep snow, mud, and other unstable surfaces without impediment.

### Luminous
Faeries emit a faint silver or golden glimmer at night. On command, they can produce a nonflammable light and direct it to move freely within their vicinity. This light illuminates up to a 30 ft. radius.

### Sense Truth
Faeries can sense the truthfulness of any statement made within 10 ft. of them. This ability detects sincerity, not objective fact — if the speaker genuinely believes what they are saying, it registers as true regardless of whether it is. A deliberate lie registers immediately. +2 on Opposition Checks to detect or resist deception, lies, or manipulation. Automatic success on Opposition Checks to detect or resist deception, lies, or manipulation when within 10 feet.

## Flaws

### Blood Feud
The hatred of Imps is not cultural — it is written into every Faerie at a level below conscious thought. What the Imps brought down on the Faerie race is not something that can be reasoned past or set aside for tactical convenience.

When a Faerie encounters an Imp, they must succeed on a Willpower Opposition Check against difficulty 15 to refrain from immediately attacking. On failure, they attack — and continue attacking until the Imp is dead or no longer present.

### Severed Bond
Luniel stripped the mate bond from the Faerie race as punishment for their silence. Every Faerie alive was born into the absence of it. They know it only from their ancestors' accounts — the shape of something that should be there and isn't. This is not a wound that heals.

−2 on Willpower Opposition Checks to resist emotional manipulation or social pressure that exploits loneliness, longing, or the desire for connection.

### Touch of Iron
Iron is antithetical to faerie nature. Any weapon made of iron or steel deals one additional damage die on a hit.

### Transparent
A Faerie cannot bluff, misdirect, or hold a false position. This is the same constitutional fact that makes them trustworthy — seen from the other side. In any negotiation or social exchange where a false front, a strategic lie, or deliberate misdirection would be the natural approach, a Faerie is working without the most common tool in the kit.

−2 on Negotiation Opposition Checks. A Faerie cannot attempt deception-based social tactics under any circumstances.

### Walking Target
A Faerie's natural luminescence is faint enough to pass unremarked in daylight — but at night, they are a lantern with legs. −4 on Stealth Opposition Checks at night or in darkness.
