# Tuvien's Echo

- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 2 AP
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One object or location
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster touches an object or a fixed point in a location and perceives a brief vision — an echo of the most emotionally significant moment that occurred there. The vision is not a full account. It is what the place or object remembers most deeply: a fragment of feeling, an impression of presence, the shape of something that mattered.

The Narrator determines what the echo shows, based on the history of the object or location. The caster cannot direct or control what surfaces. The Narrator should favor feeling over fact — what the place remembers, not what a witness would report. The echo cannot be rewound, replayed, or extended.
