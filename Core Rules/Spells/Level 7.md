# Level 7 Spells

*Level 7 spells cost 7 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting VIII.*


### Balathor's Wrath

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Offense, Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** A point within range; all creatures within 30 ft. of that point
- **Damage:** 2d10 Fire (1d6 Fire per turn, 3 rounds)
- **Duration:** 3 rounds
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster calls down an explosive burst of fire centered on the target point. Every creature within 30 ft. must make a Dodge Opposition Check against the caster's Spellcasting dice pool + Willpower. On a failed defense, the target takes 2d10 fire damage. On a successful defense, they take half damage.

The burst sets the ground alight. For 3 rounds, any creature that enters or begins their turn in the affected area takes 1d6 fire damage. The fire can be extinguished by Nunor's Blessing or similar effects.

Balathor does not do precise.

### Elemental Bolt VII

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 4d6 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 4d6 damage of the chosen type.


### Tree of Life

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** A point the caster can see within 30 ft.
- **Duration:** Up to 10 rounds

The caster summons a golden, ethereal tree no larger than a human being at the chosen point. When it appears, the Narrator sets the tree's **Healing Pool** to the caster's Willpower score plus 7.

On the round it appears, the tree releases a healing burst: every ally in its line of sight recovers Health equal to half the Healing Pool (rounded down). The Healing Pool is then reduced by that amount.

At the end of each subsequent round, every ally in the tree's line of sight recovers 4 Health, and 4 is subtracted from the Healing Pool. When the Healing Pool reaches 0, or when 10 rounds have elapsed, the tree vanishes.

The tree's healing cannot pass through solid walls or objects. Allies who move out of the tree's line of sight do not receive healing that round; those who return regain the benefit. The tree is fixed in place.

The spell requires no Concentration. The tree persists independently of the caster's position or condition.


### Tuvien's Veil

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** The caster and all allies within 30 ft.
- **Duration:** Concentration

The caster draws a veil around themselves and all allies within range. For as long as Concentration is maintained, veiled creatures cannot be perceived by any magical means — scrying, Far Sight, divination, and similar effects find nothing where they are. The veil moves with the caster; allies who move beyond 30 ft. leave it, and those who return within range rejoin it.

The veil conceals presence only. It does not affect mundane sight, sound, or physical detection.
