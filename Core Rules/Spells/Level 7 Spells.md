# Level 7 Spells

*Level 7 spells cost 7 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting VIII.*


### Balathor's Wrath

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Offense, Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** A point within range
- **Damage:** 2d10 Fire (1d6 Fire per turn, 3 rounds)
- **Duration:** 3 rounds
- **Defense:** Dodge ([dice pool](../Encounters/Dice%20Pools.md) + Reflex)

The caster calls down an explosive burst of fire centered on the target point. Every creature within 30 ft. must make a Dodge Opposition Check against the caster's Spellcasting [dice pool](../Encounters/Dice%20Pools.md) + Willpower. On a failed defense, the target takes 2d10 fire damage. On a successful defense, they take half damage (1d10 fire damage).

The burst sets the ground alight. For 3 rounds, any creature that enters or begins their turn in the affected area takes 1d6 fire damage. The fire can be extinguished by Nunor's Blessing or similar effects.

Balathor does not do precise.

### Elemental Bolt VII

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 4d6 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge ([dice pool](../Encounters/Dice%20Pools.md) + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 4d6 damage of the chosen type.


### Tree of Life

- **Level:** 7
- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 3 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 7 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 10 ft.
- **Target:** A point the caster can see within 30 ft.
- **Duration:** Up to 10 rounds

The caster summons a golden, ethereal tree no larger than a human being at the chosen point. When it appears, the Narrator sets the tree's **Healing Pool** to the caster's Willpower score plus 20.

On the round it appears, the tree releases a healing burst: every ally within 10 ft. of the tree recovers 8 Health. The Healing Pool is then reduced by 8.

At the end of each subsequent round, every ally within 10 ft. of the tree recovers 4 Health, and 4 is subtracted from the Healing Pool. If less than 4 points remain in the tree's Healing Pool, every ally within 10 ft. of the tree recovers an amount of Health equal to the remaining points in the Healing Pool. The Healing Pool is then exhausted.

When the Healing Pool reaches 0, or when 10 rounds have elapsed, the tree vanishes.

The tree's healing cannot pass through solid walls or objects. Allies who move beyond 10 ft. of the tree do not receive healing that round; those who return within range regain the benefit. The tree is fixed in place.

The spell requires no Concentration. The tree persists independently of the caster's position or condition.
