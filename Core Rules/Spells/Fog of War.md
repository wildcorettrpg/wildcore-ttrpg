# Fog of War

- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 0 AP
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** A location within range
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster summons a thin fog that spreads in a 30 ft. radius from the target location. The fog flows around obstacles and down unobstructed hallways and passages. Obstructed corridors block its spread.

All individuals within the fog suffer −4 to Attack Opposition Checks. The caster may dismiss the fog at any time at no cost.

This spell cannot be charged or overclocked. It always produces the effect above, cast as a Free Action with no AP cost and no effect on the Predation Clock.
