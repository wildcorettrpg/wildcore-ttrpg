# Level 4 Spells

*Level 4 spells cost 4 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting V.*


### Balathor's Mantle

- **Level:** 4
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 4 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** Self
- **Damage:** 1d8 Fire (melee attackers)
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster wraps themselves in a cloak of living fire. For as long as the mantle persists, any creature that strikes the caster with a melee attack takes 1d8 fire damage.

The mantle sheds light in a 20 ft. radius. It does not harm the caster or their equipment. The caster may dismiss the mantle at any time at no cost.

### Elemental Bolt IV

- **Level:** 4
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 4 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 1d12 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 1d12 damage of the chosen type.


### Inoen's Favor

- **Level:** 4
- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 4 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster lays a hand on the target, who recovers 1d8 Health.


### Luniel's Blessing

- **Level:** 4
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 4 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** Self
- **Duration:** Special

The caster calls on Luniel, Goddess of Life, summoning an ethereal creature to fight at their side. The caster chooses the creature's form at cast time from the animal shapes available to a Shapeshifter (see [Shapeshifter Lineage](Shapeshifter%20Lineage.md)). The creature appears adjacent to the caster and acts on the caster's turn, following their commands.

The creature's Vitality is equal to half the caster's current Vitality score, rounded down. It uses the stat block for its chosen animal form. The creature is vulnerable to magic and iron weapons — attacks of either type deal double damage to it. It cannot be healed by any means.

When the creature's Health reaches 0, it vanishes. The caster may also dismiss it at any time at no cost.


### Tuvien's Mercy

- **Level:** 4
- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 4 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature that died within the last round
- **Duration:** 1 hour

The caster reaches across the threshold and holds a recently dead creature at the boundary between life and death. The target is stable and unconscious — not alive, not yet gone. Death does not take them for 1 hour.

This is not resurrection. The target will die when the hour expires unless they receive sufficient healing to bring them above 0 Health before then.
