# Ellessien's Breath

- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 0 AP
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** A 5 ft. square, person, creature, or object
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster summons a small gust of air at the target's location, sufficient to knock over a Tiny object.

This spell cannot be charged or overclocked. It always produces the effect above, cast as a Free Action with no AP cost and no effect on the Predation Clock.
