# Level 9 Spells

*Level 9 spells cost 9 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting X.*


### Elemental Bolt IX

- **Level:** 9
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 9 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 4d10 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 4d10 damage of the chosen type.


### Nunor's Trident

- **Level:** 9
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 9 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

A glowing ethereal trident, ten feet tall, appears hovering above the battlefield. It rings once — a deep, resonant tone felt as much as heard — and vanishes. The call carries into the depths of the nearest body of water, or into the Magesea itself if no surface water is near.

What answers is Nunor's decision, not the caster's. It may be a single leviathan. It may be many smaller things. It may be something the caster has no name for. The creatures fight alongside the party until the encounter ends or they are slain, then return to the depths without ceremony.

The caster does not control what comes. They have Nunor's attention for exactly as long as it takes him to decide what to send. He does not consult them. He does not hold back.

### True Sight

- **Level:** 9
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 9 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft (line of sight)
- **Target:** One person or creature
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster perceives four truths about the target simultaneously:

- **Disposition** — how the target genuinely regards the caster and their allies, beneath any social mask, performance, or illusion (hostile, wary, neutral, friendly, devoted, or any shade between).
- **Motivation** — the core drive that governs the target's choices and actions above all else.
- **Intention** — what the target means to do next, or within the current scene.
- **Essence** — whether the target is of mundane origin or carries something beyond it: magical, demonic, celestial, undead, or any other extraordinary nature they bear.

These truths are perceived directly, not inferred. Shapeshifting, glamours, illusions, and deliberate deception cannot obscure them. The target is unaware the spell was cast.


### Tuvien's Answer

- **Level:** 9
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 9 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** Self
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster asks one question aloud. Tuvien answers it. The answer arrives as a direct knowing — not a vision, not a voice, but a certainty that was not there before. It is true.

The question may concern any person, place, event, or matter — past or present. It may not concern the future. Tuvien is bound by his office; he cannot reveal what has not yet occurred.

If the caster is unwilling to hear the answer — if some part of them is choosing not to know — the spell fails. The 9 Health is spent regardless.
