# Ellessien's Ward

- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 0 AP
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Until consumed

The caster lays a hand on the target, raising a shimmering barrier of highly compressed air around them. The ward absorbs the next 1d6 points of damage the target would take, then vanishes.

This spell cannot be charged or overclocked. It always produces the effect above, cast as a Free Action with no AP cost and no effect on the Predation Clock.
