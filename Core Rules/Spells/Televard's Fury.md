# Televard's Fury

- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 8 AP
- **Range:** 30 ft.
- **Radius:** 30 ft. cone
- **Target:** All creatures within radius
- **Damage:** 4d12 Fire (2d12 on successful defense)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge

The caster releases a rolling wall of fire that tears through everything in a 30 ft. cone. Targets outside the cone are unaffected. Targets within the cone make a Dodge Opposition Check against the caster's [Spellcasting attack pool](../Core%20Mechanics/Dice%20Pools.md) — the result determines how badly the blast catches them.

On a failed defense, the target takes 4d12 fire damage. On a successful defense, they take half damage (2d12 fire damage).
