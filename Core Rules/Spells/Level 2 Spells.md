# Level 2 Spells

*Level 2 spells cost 2 [Health](../Characters/Health.md) to cast. They are unlocked at [Spellcasting III](../Skills/Universal/Combat/Spellcasting%20Skill.md).*


### Elemental Bolt II

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 1d8 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 1d8 damage of the chosen type.


### Balathor's Brand

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 1d4 Fire (ongoing, per turn)
- **Duration:** Until extinguished
- **Defense:** Dodge

The caster touches the target and sets them alight with magical fire. On a failed defense, the target catches fire and takes 1d4 fire damage at the end of each of their turns until the fire is extinguished.

The fire can be extinguished by spending 1 AP to stop, drop, and smother it; by submersion in water; or by any effect that explicitly extinguishes fire.

### Enduril's Binds

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Concentration
- **Defense:** Dodge

Ethereal binds erupt from the ground and wrap around the target. On a failed defense, the target is [Incapacitated](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#incapacitated) for as long as the caster maintains Concentration. The binds vanish the moment Concentration ends.


### Kerek's Cloak

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** A point within range
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster calls on Kerek, God of Dark, plunging a 30 ft. radius around the target point into absolute darkness. No natural or magical light source can illuminate the area for the duration.

Any creature within the darkness that relies on sight to perceive is treated as [Blind](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#blind)ed. Creatures with abilities that allow them to perceive without light are unaffected. The caster may dismiss the darkness at any time at no cost.


### Rasgard's Bolts

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1–5 AP (chosen at cast time)
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health per AP spent
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature per bolt; bolts may be distributed freely among targets in range or concentrated on one
- **Damage:** 1d8 Magical Force (unmitigated) per bolt
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** None

The caster releases one bolt of pure magical force per AP spent. Each bolt unerringly finds its target — no attack Opposition Check is made and no defense is possible. Bolts are directed individually at the moment of casting; a caster spending 3 AP could send all three at a single enemy or split them across three.

**Predation Clock.** This spell subtracts the total Health cost paid (2 per bolt) from the Predation Clock rather than the spell's base level. A 5-AP cast subtracts 10.


### Solan's Light

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One person, creature, or object
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The target becomes a light source, illuminating everything within 30 ft. as if it were broad daylight. The caster may dismiss the effect at any time at no cost.


### Tuvien's Echo

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 2 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One object or location
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster touches an object or a fixed point in a location and perceives a brief vision — an echo of the most emotionally significant moment that occurred there. The vision is not a full account. It is what the place or object remembers most deeply: a fragment of feeling, an impression of presence, the shape of something that mattered.

The Narrator determines what the echo shows, based on the history of the object or location. The caster cannot direct or control what surfaces. The Narrator should favor feeling over fact — what the place remembers, not what a witness would report. The echo cannot be rewound, replayed, or extended.
