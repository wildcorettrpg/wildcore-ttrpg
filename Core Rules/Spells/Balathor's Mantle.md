# Balathor's Mantle

- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 4 AP
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** Self
- **Damage:** 1d8 Fire (melee attackers)
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster wraps themselves in a cloak of living fire. For as long as the mantle persists, any creature that strikes the caster with a melee attack takes 1d8 fire damage.

The mantle sheds light in a 20 ft. radius. It does not harm the caster or their equipment. The caster may dismiss the mantle at any time at no cost.
