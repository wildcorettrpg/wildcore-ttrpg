# Level 5 Spells

*Level 5 spells cost 5 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting VI.*


### Elemental Bolt V

- **Level:** 5
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 5 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 4d4 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 4d4 damage of the chosen type.


### Inoen's Salve

- **Level:** 5
- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 5 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster lays a hand on the target. The Poisoned condition ends immediately.


### Nunor's Favor

- **Level:** 5
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 5 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** One square
- **Duration:** 8 hours

A dome of solid ice rises from the target point in a 10 ft. radius. The dome is impervious to mundane and magical attack, cannot be breached by force or spell, and blocks all sound and psychic effects from passing through its walls in either direction — no noise escapes, no noise enters, and mind-affecting spells cannot reach across the boundary.

At the center of the dome, a spring of fresh water rises from the ground for the duration.

Those within the dome when it is cast may leave and re-enter freely. The dome cannot be entered or breached from the outside by any means. When the eight hours expire, the dome dissolves and the spring goes dry.

Nunor does not provide shelter because he cares. He provides it because he was asked, and because the alternative was being ignored.


### Tuvien's Bond

- **Level:** 5
- **Category:** Defense, Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 5 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** Two willing creatures
- **Duration:** Concentration

The caster touches two willing creatures and binds them together. For as long as the caster maintains Concentration, any damage taken by either bonded creature is divided evenly between both — rounded up to the creature originally struck for odd amounts.

The bond makes both creatures more resilient against any single attack, and more vulnerable to the cumulative weight of many. It cannot be forced on an unwilling creature. Either bonded creature may ask the caster to end it at any time.

### Kerek's Reckoning

- **Level:** 5
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 5 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Willpower (dice pool + Willpower)

The caster names a single subject and opens Kerek's eye upon the target's mind. On a failed defense, the target is forced to see the truth of that subject with absolute clarity — stripped of rationalization, self-deception, and illusion. They cannot look away from what is shown to them.

The spell compels sight, not action. What the target does with the truth is entirely their own reckoning.
