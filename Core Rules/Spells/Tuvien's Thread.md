# Tuvien's Thread

- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** Two willing creatures
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster touches two willing creatures, and an invisible thread forms between them. For as long as the thread persists, each creature always knows the direction and approximate distance to the other — through walls, across any distance, regardless of magical concealment or planar barriers.

The thread conveys location only, not condition, thought, or sensation. Either creature may dismiss it at any time at no cost.
