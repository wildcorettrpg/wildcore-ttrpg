# Level 6 Spells

*Level 6 spells cost 6 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting VII.*


### Elemental Bolt VI

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 2d10 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 2d10 damage of the chosen type.


### Far Sight

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** Known target (any distance)
- **Target:** One known person, place, or object
- **Duration:** Concentration

The caster's perception reaches across any distance to a target they have personally encountered, know by name, or hold a meaningful connection to. A vision opens: the caster sees the target and everything within 30 ft. of it as it exists at this moment. Far Sight is anchored to the present — it cannot show the past or the future.

The vision begins as a broad impression — general surroundings, time of day, whether the target is present and in motion. Each round of sustained Concentration brings it into sharper focus: the faces of others present, the contents of a room, objects on a surface, written words. The Narrator determines what becomes discernible as the caster continues to watch.

Far Sight conveys sight only. Sound, smell, and other physical senses do not carry across the vision.

The caster cannot perceive their own immediate surroundings for the duration. The target is unaware the spell was cast. Magical wards or concealment effects that specifically block scrying end the vision immediately.


### Inoen's Blessing

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** All allies in the caster's line of sight, up to 30 ft.
- **Duration:** Concentration

The caster raises a barrier around each ally in their line of sight, up to 30 ft. away. While protected, allies are shielded from environmental hazards — hazard damage does not apply and Fortitude checks triggered by environmental conditions are not required. Allies who move out of line of sight or beyond 30 ft. lose the protection; those who return within range regain it.

The barrier moves with the caster.


### Ordred's Respite

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Crowd Control, Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** 20 ft.
- **Target:** The caster and all allies within 20 ft.
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster calls on Ordred, God of Earth, and a wall of stone erupts from the ground in a 20 ft. radius around them, enclosing the caster and all allies within range. The wall's surface is sheer and cannot be scaled.

In an enclosed space, the wall rises from floor to ceiling, sealing the enclosure completely. Outdoors, the wall ascends 1,000 feet into the air.

The wall is impenetrable. Nothing passes through it in either direction for the duration. The caster may dismiss the wall at any time at no cost.


### Rasgard's Rage

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Offense, Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** 60 ft.
- **Target:** All creatures within a 60 ft. cone
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster releases a burst of concussive force that tears through everything in a 60 ft. cone. Targets outside the cone are unaffected. Targets within the cone make a Dodge Opposition Check against the caster's Spellcasting dice pool + Willpower — the result determines how badly the blast catches them.

On a successful defense, the target is Stunned for 1 round. On a failed defense, the target is knocked Prone and Stunned for 2 rounds.
