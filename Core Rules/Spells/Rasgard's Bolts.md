# Rasgard's Bolts

- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1–5 AP (chosen at cast time)
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature per bolt; bolts may be distributed freely among targets in range or concentrated on one
- **Damage:** 1d8 Magical Force (unmitigated) per bolt
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** None

The caster releases one bolt of pure magical force per AP spent. Each bolt unerringly finds its target — no attack Opposition Check is made and no defense is possible. Bolts are directed individually at the moment of casting; a caster spending 3 AP could send all three at a single enemy or split them across three.

This spell's number of bolts is determined directly by AP spent — it does not use the standard [Charging](../Magic/Magic.md#potency-and-charging) multiplier, and cannot be charged further beyond choosing how much AP to commit at the moment of casting. Predation Clock drain follows the normal rule: the total AP spent is subtracted from the Clock.
