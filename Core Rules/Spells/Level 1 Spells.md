# Level 1 Spells

*Level 1 spells cost 1 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting II.*


### Elemental Bolt I

- **Level:** 1
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 1 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 1d6 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 1d6 damage of the chosen type.


### Inoen's Balm

- **Level:** 1
- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 1 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster lays a hand on the target, who recovers 1d4 Health.


### Luniel's Garden

- **Level:** 1
- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 1 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 15 ft.
- **Target:** A point within range
- **Duration:** Until dismissed
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

A circle of grasping vines erupts from the ground in a 15 ft. radius around the target point. Creatures within the area that fail their defense cannot move for as long as they remain within the vines. Affected creatures may still act normally — they can attack, cast spells, and take other actions, but cannot leave their current position.

Creatures that succeed their defense are unaffected. Creatures that enter the area after the spell is cast must make the same defense or be caught. The caster may dismiss the vines at any time at no cost.


### Nunor's Ward

- **Level:** 1
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 1 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Radius:** 10 ft.
- **Target:** All creatures within radius
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

A wall of swirling seawater rises around the caster in a 10 ft. radius, churning with enough force to deflect and obscure incoming attacks. All enemies attacking creatures within the ward suffer −2 to their attack Opposition Checks. The ward moves with the caster.

The wall does not impede movement in or out of the radius. It provides no protection against spells. The caster may dismiss the ward at any time at no cost.

### Tuvien's Thread

- **Level:** 1
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 1 Health
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** Two willing creatures
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster touches two willing creatures, and an invisible thread forms between them. For as long as the thread persists, each creature always knows the direction and approximate distance to the other — through walls, across any distance, regardless of magical concealment or planar barriers.

The thread conveys location only, not condition, thought, or sensation. Either creature may dismiss it at any time at no cost.

### Whisper Walk

- **Level:** 1
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 1 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The target's movement produces no sound for the duration. Stealth Opposition Checks against the target that rely solely on auditory detection automatically succeed. Visual detection is unaffected.

The spell is cast on a target within 30 ft., but the target may move up to 120 ft. from the caster without breaking the effect, provided they remain within the caster's line of sight.
