# Spells

Spells are supernatural effects unlocked by the [Spellcasting](../Skills/Universal/Combat/Spellcasting.md) skill tree. For rules on casting, spell attacks, defense, Health cost, and overcharging, see [Magic](../Encounters/Magic.md).
