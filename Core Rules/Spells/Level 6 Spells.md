# Level 6 Spells

*Level 6 spells cost 6 [Health](../Characters/Health.md) to cast. They are unlocked at [Spellcasting VII](../Skills/Universal/Combat/Spellcasting%20Skill.md).*


### Elemental Bolt VI

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 2d10 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 2d10 damage of the chosen type.


### Inoen's Blessing

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Radius:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** All allies within radius
- **Duration:** Concentration

The caster raises a barrier around each ally within 30 ft. While protected, allies are shielded from environmental hazards — hazard damage does not apply and Fortitude checks triggered by environmental conditions are not required. Allies who move out of line of sight or beyond 30 ft. lose the protection; those who return within range regain it.

The barrier moves with the caster.


### Ordred's Respite

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Crowd Control, Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Radius:** 20 ft.
- **Target:** The caster and all allies within radius
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster calls on Ordred, God of Earth, and a wall of stone erupts from the ground in a 20 ft. radius around them, enclosing the caster and all allies within range. The wall's surface is sheer and cannot be scaled.

In an enclosed space, the wall rises from floor to ceiling, sealing the enclosure completely. Outdoors, the wall ascends 1,000 feet into the air.

The wall is impenetrable. Nothing passes through it in either direction for the duration. The caster may dismiss the wall at any time at no cost.


### Rasgard's Rage

- **Level:** 6
- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 6 Health
- **Range:** 60 ft.
- **Radius:** 60 ft. cone
- **Target:** All creatures within radius
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge

The caster releases a burst of concussive force that tears through everything in a 60 ft. cone. Targets outside the cone are unaffected. Targets within the cone make a Dodge Opposition Check against the caster's [Spellcasting attack pool](../Core%20Mechanics/Dice%20Pools.md) — the result determines how badly the blast catches them.

On a successful defense, the target is [Stunned](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#stunned) for 1 round. On a failed defense, the target is knocked [Prone](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#prone) and [Stunned](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#stunned) for 2 rounds.
