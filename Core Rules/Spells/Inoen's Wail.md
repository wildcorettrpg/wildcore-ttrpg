# Inoen's Wail

- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 8 AP
- **Range:** Self
- **Radius:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** All enemies within radius
- **Duration:** Concentration
- **Defense:** Willpower

The caster opens a channel to Inoen, Goddess of Death, and lets her grief pour into the minds of all enemies within range — the wailing of the fallen, the bereft, the innocent dead. The sound exists only in their minds; it is not otherwise audible. It is a sound that drives men to their knees.

All enemies who fail their defense are [Incapacitated](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#incapacitated) for as long as the caster maintains Concentration. The effect is centered on the caster and moves with them. Any enemy who succeeds their defense is unaffected and may act normally.
