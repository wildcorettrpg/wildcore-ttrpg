# Level 0 Spells

*Level 0 spells cost no Health to cast and do not affect the Predation Difficulty. They are unlocked at Spellcasting I.*

### Balathor's Gift

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** A point or surface
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster summons a flame from nothing. At cast time, they choose the size — anywhere from a candle to a campfire. The flame is real: it produces heat and light, and will spread to flammable materials if left unattended. The caster may dismiss it at any time at no cost.

Balathor gives fire freely. What you do with it is your business.

### Balathor's Jest

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 1d4 Fire
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster touches a single target, igniting a brief burst of fire against them. On a failed defense, the target takes 1d4 fire damage.

### Ellessien's Breath

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** A 5 ft. square, person, creature, or object
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster summons a small gust of air at the target's location, sufficient to knock over a Tiny object.

### Ellessien's Ward

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Defense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Duration:** Until consumed

The caster lays a hand on the target, raising a shimmering barrier of highly compressed air around them. The ward absorbs the next 1d6 points of damage the target would take, then vanishes.

### Fog of War

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** A location within range
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster summons a thin fog that spreads in a 30 ft. radius from the target location. The fog flows around obstacles and down unobstructed hallways and passages. Obstructed corridors block its spread.

All individuals within the fog suffer −4 to Attack Opposition Checks. The caster may dismiss the fog at any time at no cost.

### Nunor's Spring

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** A point on the ground within range
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The caster calls on Nunor and clean water rises from the earth at the target point — enough to fill a waterskin, water a small flock, or serve a handful of travelers. The water is fresh regardless of the ground it rises from. It does not continue to flow once it has surfaced.

Nunor does not acknowledge the casting. The water simply comes.

### Malavard's Ward

- **Level:** 0
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** None
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One object, door, or window
- **Duration:** Until dismissed

The caster touches a mundane object, door, or window and speaks a password of their choosing. A magical ward settles over the target — it cannot be opened or moved by any means unless the correct password is spoken aloud. The caster must declare the password at the time of casting.

The ward has no effect on magical objects or living creatures. Speaking the correct password dispels the ward
