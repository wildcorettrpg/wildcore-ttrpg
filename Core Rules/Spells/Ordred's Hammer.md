# Ordred's Hammer

- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 3 AP
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One person, creature, or object
- **Damage:** 4d6 Magical Force (unmitigated)
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Defense:** None (strikes automatically — no attack Opposition Check)

The caster summons an ethereal hammer in the likeness of Ordred's forge hammer. When thrown, it strikes its target automatically — no attack Opposition Check is required — dealing 4d6 Magical Force damage. The hammer is a conjured weapon of pure supernatural energy; armor mitigation does not apply. Upon striking, the hammer vanishes. If not thrown within 1 round of being summoned, it vanishes without effect.
