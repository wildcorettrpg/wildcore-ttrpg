# Tree of Life

- **Category:** Healing
- **Casting Time:** 3 AP
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 10 ft.
- **Target:** A point the caster can see within 30 ft.
- **Duration:** Up to 10 rounds

The caster summons a golden, ethereal tree no larger than a human being at the chosen point. When it appears, the Narrator sets the tree's **Healing Pool** to the caster's Willpower score plus 5.

On the round it appears, the tree releases a healing burst: every ally within radius recovers 2 Health, and the Healing Pool is reduced by that amount.

At the end of each subsequent round, every ally within radius recovers 1 Health, and the Healing Pool is reduced by that amount. If less than that amount remains in the Healing Pool, every ally instead recovers Health equal to whatever remains, and the Healing Pool is then exhausted.

When the Healing Pool reaches 0, or when the spell's Duration elapses, the tree vanishes.

The tree's healing cannot pass through solid walls or objects. Allies who move beyond the tree's radius do not receive healing that round; those who return within range regain the benefit. The tree is fixed in place.

Once summoned, the tree persists independently of the caster's position or condition and requires no Concentration to maintain.

As with any spell, charging this beyond its base Casting Time multiplies every numerical value here — Healing Pool, burst, per-round recovery, radius, and Duration all scale together. See [Potency and Charging](../Magic/Magic.md#potency-and-charging).
