# Level 8 Spells

*Level 8 spells cost 8 Health to cast. They are unlocked at Spellcasting IX.*


### Balathor's Creation

- **Level:** 8
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 8 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** A point adjacent to the caster
- **Duration:** Until all Bolg are slain

The caster calls on Balathor, who reaches into his endless workshop and releases one of his favorite experiments.

1d6 Bolg appear adjacent to the caster. They are real — not summoned, not ethereal. Balathor made them, and he sent them here. They cannot be dismissed.

**The Bolg**

Bolg are 1.5 ft. tall, hairless, pale, and built almost entirely around their mouths. Their heads are dominated by rows of razor-sharp teeth set into jaws that hinge wider than their skull should allow. They have no eyes. They hunt by scent, and they can smell blood through skin, scale, and hide.

They are hive creatures. Individually, they are small. Together, they have killed dragons.

**How They Fight**

Each Bolg acts on the caster's turn. They fight with one goal: find the most wounded thing in their priority order and kill it.

Their priority is as follows: enemies first, working through the healthiest enemy until all enemies are down. When no enemies remain, they turn to horses, pack animals, and other non-combatant creatures present. When those are gone, they turn to party members, starting with the healthiest. _Bolg crave blood._

They do not stop. The encounter ending does not stop them. The caster's wishes do not stop them. Balathor sent them here to do a job, and they are going to do it until there is nothing left to do it to or until they are dead.

The Bolg swarm their current target together. A bolg has a bite dealing 2d4 damage and two claws dealing 1d6, each. If any attack should get past armor mitigation, the target immediately enters the Bleeding Out condition. Multiple successful hits stack the Bleeding Out condition on the target.

A single Bolg has 15 Vitality and naturally tough hide that serves as armor, providing 9 damage mitigation. Their base movement speed is 60 ft.

Balathor is very proud of the Bolg.

### Elemental Bolt VIII

- **Level:** 8
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 8 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 3d10 Fire, Ice, or Electric
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster chooses a damage type — Fire, Ice, or Electric — at the moment of casting. The bolt strikes its target with elemental force, dealing 3d10 damage of the chosen type.


### Inoen's Wail

- **Level:** 8
- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 8 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Target:** All enemies within 30 ft.
- **Duration:** Concentration
- **Defense:** Willpower (dice pool + Willpower)

The caster opens a channel to Inoen, Goddess of Death, and lets her grief pour into the minds of all enemies within range — the wailing of the fallen, the bereft, the innocent dead. The sound exists only in their minds; it is not otherwise audible. It is a sound that drives men to their knees.

All enemies who fail their defense are Incapacitated for as long as the caster maintains Concentration. The effect is centered on the caster and moves with them. Any enemy who succeeds their defense is unaffected and may act normally.

### Long Sight

- **Level:** 8
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 8 Health
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** One question about a specific concern
- **Duration:** Concentration (hours or longer)

The caster names a question about a specific concern and enters the vision. Long Sight does not answer the question — it shows the caster all of time as it touches the subject.

The past is fixed. The caster may observe any moment that has already occurred with complete clarity — what was said, what was done, what was present. The present is equally accessible, functioning as Far Sight does, but without its limitation to a known and connected subject.

The future is different. Time ahead is not a single line but an infinite weave of threads, each vying to pass through the eye of a needle and become the past. Looking far ahead reveals countless threads, most of which will never survive to become real. Looking close to the present reveals fewer — each more probable than the last. One thread, and only one, will pass through.

As the caster maintains Concentration, future visions narrow. Less probable threads fade first. What remains grows clearer with each passing hour. A vision held long enough shows only threads with a strong claim on the present — but even these are not guaranteed. None of them are.

Long Sight is most dangerous when acted on too early. A caster who treats a distant vision as prophecy may act on a thread that was never going to survive — abandoning a path that would have led somewhere better, or bringing about the very outcome they sought to avoid. The Narrator determines what the caster sees and when threads fade, based on the nature of the question and events unfolding in the world.

The caster cannot perceive their own immediate surroundings for the duration.


### Televard's Fury

- **Level:** 8
- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 8 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** All creatures in a 30 ft. cone
- **Damage:** 4d12 Fire (2d12 on successful defense)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge (dice pool + Reflex)

The caster releases a rolling wall of fire that tears through everything in a 30 ft. cone. Targets outside the cone are unaffected. Targets within the cone make a Dodge Opposition Check against the caster's Spellcasting dice pool + Willpower — the result determines how badly the blast catches them.

On a failed defense, the target takes 4d12 fire damage. On a successful defense, they take half damage (2d12 fire damage).


### Tuvien's Covenant

- **Level:** 8
- **Category:** Utility
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Casting Cost:** 8 Health
- **Range:** 30 ft.
- **Target:** Two willing parties
- **Duration:** Permanent until fulfilled or broken

Two parties speak an oath in Tuvien's name while the caster holds the spell. From that moment, both parties know with absolute certainty whether the other has broken the terms of the oath. The knowledge is immediate and unmistakable — not a feeling or a suspicion, but a fact, delivered directly to the mind.

The oath cannot be broken accidentally. Only deliberate choice violates it. Tuvien does not enforce consequences — he only bears witness. What follows from a broken oath belongs to the parties, not to him.
