# Balathor's Jest

- **Category:** Offense
- **Casting Time:** 0 AP
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One creature
- **Damage:** 1d4 Fire
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Defense:** Dodge

The caster touches a single target, igniting a brief burst of fire against them. On a failed defense, the target takes 1d4 fire damage.

This spell cannot be charged or overclocked. It always produces the effect above, cast as a Free Action with no AP cost and no effect on the Predation Clock.
