# Luniel's Garden

- **Category:** Crowd Control
- **Casting Time:** 1 AP
- **Range:** 30 ft. (line of sight)
- **Radius:** 15 ft.
- **Target:** A point within range
- **Duration:** Until dismissed
- **Defense:** Dodge

A circle of grasping vines erupts from the ground in a 15 ft. radius around the target point. Creatures within the area that fail their defense cannot move for as long as they remain within the vines. Affected creatures may still act normally — they can attack, cast spells, and take other actions, but cannot leave their current position.

Creatures that succeed their defense are unaffected. Creatures that enter the area after the spell is cast must make the same defense or be caught. The caster may dismiss the vines at any time at no cost.
