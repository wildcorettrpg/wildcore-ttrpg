# Stabilization

## What Stabilization Does

A character who is [Bleeding Out](Conditions.md#bleeding-out) can be stabilized by a healer. Stabilization ends **[Bleeding Out](Conditions.md#bleeding-out)** — nothing more. It does not restore Health, and it does not remove the [Unconscious](Conditions.md#unconscious) condition. A stabilized character at 7 Health or below may still make Consciousness Checks if they take further damage — Stabilization addresses the wound, not the character's current Health.

## Requirements

To attempt stabilization, the healer must be adjacent to the patient and must spend an action on the attempt.

**Healing Opposition Check.** The Narrator assigns a difficulty from the [Opposition Check Difficulty Table](../Tables/Opposition%20Check%20Difficulty%20Table.md) based on the severity of the patient's condition — typically Challenging (8–13) for a stable situation outside combat, and Dangerous (14–16) in active combat or for a patient close to death.

The healer's roll is **1d10** (plus any additional dice granted by Healing skills), plus flat bonuses from Healing skills (Healing I, II, V, and VI each grant **+1**). Using a Healer's Bag item during the attempt adds **+2** to the roll; using a Master Healer's Satchel item adds **+4**. Consuming an item in either case uses one item from the bag or satchel.

## Failure

If the healer fails the Opposition Check, the character is not stabilized. A failed attempt does not worsen the character's condition, and the healer may try again on a subsequent turn.

## After Stabilization

A stabilized character remains at their current Health. They can still take damage normally. If they were [Unconscious](Conditions.md#unconscious) when stabilized, they remain [Unconscious](Conditions.md#unconscious) until the [Unconscious](Conditions.md#unconscious) condition is separately removed — stabilization does not wake them.

## Forced Awakening

A character with **Healing II** or higher can perform a Forced Awakening on an [Unconscious](Conditions.md#unconscious) character using smelling salts or an equivalent stimulant. The healer must be adjacent to the patient. Forced Awakening costs **1 AP** and requires no Opposition Check. It may only be attempted once per patient per incident.

Forced Awakening removes the **[Unconscious](Conditions.md#unconscious)** condition — the character regains consciousness immediately. All other conditions present at the time of awakening remain in effect; a character who was [Bleeding Out](Conditions.md#bleeding-out) continues to be [Bleeding Out](Conditions.md#bleeding-out). The awakened character is **[Prone](Conditions.md#prone)** and **[Hindered](Conditions.md#hindered)** until they receive medical treatment or the scene ends. Forced Awakening does not restore Health.

If the awakened character's Health is still at 7 or below, they must begin making [Consciousness Checks](Dying.md#consciousness-checks) again each time their Health drops further — from [Bleeding Out](Conditions.md#bleeding-out) ticks, further damage, or any other source. Forced Awakening restores consciousness; it does not secure it.
