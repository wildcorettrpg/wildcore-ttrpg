# Poisons

## Overview

Poisons are resolved as **Fortitude checks against a static difficulty** — the same model as Environmental Hazards. The poison sets the difficulty; the character rolls to resist it.

A character exposed to a poison is immediately subjected to the [Poisoned](Conditions.md#poisoned) condition, which defines the round-by-round mechanics. The poison's severity tier determines the check difficulty and the rate of Health loss.

## Delivery Types

- **Contact.** The poison is applied to an object or surface. A creature that touches it with exposed skin suffers its effects.
- **Ingested.** The creature must swallow a full dose — delivered in food or a liquid.
- **Inhaled.** A powder or gas that takes effect when breathed in. Holding one's breath provides no protection; inhaled poisons affect mucous membranes and tear ducts.
- **Injury.** Applied to a weapon, ammunition, or trap component. The creature must take piercing or slashing damage from the coated object to be exposed.

## Severity Tiers

| Severity | Difficulty (untrained) | Difficulty (treated) | Health Loss per Round | Examples |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Mild** | 8 | 4 | 1 Health | Serpent venom, street-grade sedatives |
| **Potent** | 14 | 7 | 1d4 Health | Assassin-grade toxins, exotic animal venoms |
| **Lethal** | 20 | 10 | 2d6 Health | Refined creature toxins, alchemical murder tools |

## Notes

All poisons are Tiny, and multiple doses of the same poison stack in a single inventory slot (see [Inventory](../Equipment/Inventory.md) §Stacking). Poisons in the Named Poisons table are available as Open Game Content from the d20 SRD; they appear here in Velocity terms. Special conditions listed in the Notes column apply as stated in each entry — when no trigger is specified, the condition applies immediately on exposure and lasts until the poison ends.

The Narrator may introduce original poisons using any severity tier and delivery type; named poison entries serve as reference points for calibrating new ones.

A character with **Healing V** or higher may treat a [Poisoned](Conditions.md#poisoned) character (**1 AP**, one item consumed from the Healer's Bag). On treatment, the Fortitude check difficulty is halved for the remainder of the scene — use the Difficulty (treated) column. A single treatment application covers the scene; the healer does not need to repeat the action each round.
