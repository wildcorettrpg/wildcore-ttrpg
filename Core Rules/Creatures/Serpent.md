## Attacks

**Defender options:** Dodge, Parry, or Block

| Strike | Damage |

| Bite | 1d8 |

**Constrict (1 AP):** May be used immediately following a successful Bite against the same target. The target makes a Strength OC against difficulty 12. On failure, the target is [Restrained](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#restrained) and takes 1d8 crushing damage at the start of each of the Serpent's turns. A [Restrained](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#restrained) target may attempt to break free on their turn (1 AP, Strength OC vs. difficulty 12).
