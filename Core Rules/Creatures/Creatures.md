# Creatures

This chapter catalogs creatures referenced in the Core Rules — primarily those summoned, created, or released by spells, and creatures used across published Velocity content.

Campaign settings expand this catalog with their own creature rosters. Narrators may also build custom creatures using the SP budget framework in [Effective Level](../Characters/Effective%20Level.md).
