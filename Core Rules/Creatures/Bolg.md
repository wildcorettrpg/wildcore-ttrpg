## Attacks

**Attack pool:** 1d10 + 2 (unarmed melee)  
**Defender options:** Dodge, Parry, or Block  
**On hit:** Each successful strike applies one stack of [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out). Multiple stacks are tracked and removed independently.

Each Bolg performs a **Multi-Strike** (1 AP) — one action that resolves three separate attack Opposition Checks against the same target: one bite and two claw strikes. Each OC is rolled independently; each hit is resolved separately for damage and Bleeding Out. The Multi-Strike counts as one action for AP purposes — the cumulative multi-attack penalty does not apply within a single Multi-Strike.

## Description

Bolg are 1.5 ft. tall, hairless, and pale, built almost entirely around their mouths. Their heads are dominated by rows of razor-sharp teeth set into jaws that hinge wider than their skull should allow. They have no eyes. They hunt by scent — they can smell blood through skin, scale, and hide.

They are hive creatures. Individually, they are small. Together, they have killed dragons.

## Behavior

Bolg prioritize targets in the following order: enemies (targeting the healthiest first until all are down), then non-combatant animals present, then party members (targeting the healthiest first).

They do not stop when an encounter ends. They do not respond to the caster's wishes. Balathor sent them to do a job, and they will do it until there is nothing left to do it to — or until they are dead.

## Notes

1. Bolg are released exclusively by [Balathor's Creation](../Spells/Level%208%20Spells.md#balathors-creation) (Level 8 spell). They cannot be summoned or dismissed by any other means.
2. Each Bolg acts on the caster's turn each round.
3. The number of Bolg released is determined at casting (roll 1d6).
