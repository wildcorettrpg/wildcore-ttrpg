## Attacks

**Attack pool:** 1d10 + 2 (unarmed melee)  
**Defender options:** Dodge, Parry, or Block

| Strike | Damage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bite | 2d10 + 2 |

**Deathroll (1 AP):** May be used immediately following a successful Bite against the same target. The target makes a Strength OC against the Crocodile's Strength pool (1d10 + 2). On failure, the target is [Restrained](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#restrained). A [Restrained](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#restrained) target may attempt to break free on their turn (1 AP, Strength OC vs. the Crocodile's Strength pool). If the Crocodile is in or adjacent to water, a failed Deathroll also drags the target beneath the surface — the target must hold their breath or begin drowning per [Environmental Hazards](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Environmental%20Hazards.md).
