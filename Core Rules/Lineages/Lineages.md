# Lineages

A **lineage** is a supernatural transformation a character acquires through play — a vampiric turning, a lycanthropic curse, a celestial bond, an infernal pact, or a marking by the fae world. It is not something chosen at character creation. It is something that happens to a character during a campaign and changes them permanently.

Lineages are distinct from **races**, which are birth ancestries. A character's race (Human, Elf, Dwarf, and so on) never changes. A character's lineage is layered on top of it — and a character may hold more than one lineage simultaneously, each developing independently through its own skill tree.

## Acquiring a Lineage

Every lineage has a specific acquisition trigger — the narrative event that initiates the transformation. No lineage is purchased at character creation. The trigger must occur in play:

| Lineage | Acquisition Trigger |
|---------|---------------------|
| [Angel](Angel%20Lineage.md) | A celestial power grants the character a divine bond or marks them for a specific purpose |
| [Demon](Demon%20Lineage.md) | The character forges a pact with an infernal power, or is marked by one without asking |
| [Fae-Touched](Fae-Touched%20Lineage.md) | The character is claimed through a Faerie bargain, consumes fae food or drink, or crosses into the fae wild and returns changed |
| [Vampire](Vampire%20Lineage.md) | The character is drained and turned by another vampire |
| [Werewolf](Werewolf%20Lineage.md) | The character is bitten or scratched by a werewolf in shifted form and survives the transformation |

After the trigger, the character gains the first skill of the lineage tree at no SP cost. All subsequent skills cost 1 SP each, purchased from the character's unspent SP pool the same way any other skill is purchased. The Narrator typically awards 1 SP for the trigger event.

## Developing a Lineage

Each lineage has a 10-skill progression tree divided into a training tier (skills I–V) and a mastery tier (skills VI–X). Skills must be purchased in order. There is no cap on how many lineage skills a character may hold — the only limit is SP.

Lineage skills stack with Universal skills. A werewolf who invests in Melee Attack is a more capable fighter in both human and shifted form. An angel who invests in Spellcasting is drawing on two distinct sources of power simultaneously.

## The Weight of a Lineage

Every lineage carries obligations alongside its gifts. Angels answer to a celestial patron. Demons are entangled with an infernal power that made its bargain for reasons of its own. Vampires must feed. Werewolves carry the beast. The Fae-Touched carry the mark of something vast and old that does not forget a claimed soul.

These obligations are not mechanical subsystems — they are narrative texture. The Narrator introduces them when they serve the story, creating pressure and complication rather than punishing the player with penalties.

## The Five Core Lineages

- [Angel](Angel%20Lineage.md) — Divine bond, celestial gifts, obligations of purpose
- [Demon](Demon%20Lineage.md) — Infernal corruption, fire and shadow, entanglement with a power that wants something
- [Fae-Touched](Fae-Touched%20Lineage.md) — Fae marking, glamour and truth-sense, iron sensitivity
- [Vampire](Vampire%20Lineage.md) — Undead predator, supernatural strength and speed, the hunger
- [Werewolf](Werewolf%20Lineage.md) — Lycanthropic curse, accelerated healing and beast form, silver sensitivity
