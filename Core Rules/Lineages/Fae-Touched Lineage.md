# Fae-Touched Lineage

**Acquisition:** The character is claimed by the fae world — through a Faerie bargain, by consuming fae food or drink, by being rescued by a faerie who left something of the fae wild behind, or by crossing into the fae and returning permanently changed. The claim is real and it is permanent. Something in the character now resonates with fae nature, and that resonance deepens as they develop it.

Fae-Touched characters are not fae. They retain their race and its nature. What they carry is a layer of fae attunement that deepens over time — and as it deepens, iron becomes increasingly uncomfortable.

## What You Gain

The mark begins with passive **truth-sense**: within 30 ft. of any speaker, the character always knows when something said is a deliberate lie. This cannot be suppressed and cannot be turned off. They also see through fae glamours automatically.

The training tier adds **Lightborne Step** (immunity to difficult terrain penalties, floating movement in natural environments) and **Minor Glamour** (sensory illusions affecting a single target). The mastery tier unlocks **Greater Glamour** (all-sense illusions affecting all targets within range), the **Fae Bargain** (magically binding exchanges that neither party can voluntarily break), and **Ethereal Form** (temporary incorporeality with enhanced healing).

At the pinnacle, fae beings are compelled by the old laws not to attack the character directly without first overcoming a Willpower check.

**Strongest abilities:** Truth-sense (always-on passive), Lightborne Step (movement), Fae Bargain (binding social mechanic), Greater Glamour (illusion), Ethereal Form (incorporeality + healing).

## What It Costs

**Iron and steel weapons deal 1 additional damage die** against Fae-Touched characters. This mirrors the Touch of Iron flaw carried by the Faerie race — the fae bond makes the body respond to iron the same way a true Faerie's does.

The fae claim also creates narrative obligations. The fae world considers the character claimed, and claimed things are subject to the old laws: they can be called upon, bargained with, and held to terms they may not fully understand.
