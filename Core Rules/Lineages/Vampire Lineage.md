# Vampire Lineage

**Acquisition:** The character is drained and turned by another vampire — a process that kills and remakes the body as an undead predator. They no longer age, require food or water, or need to breathe. They are faster, stronger, and more resilient than they were in life. They are also permanently hungry.

The vampiric transformation is complete and irreversible. The character retains their memories, personality, and identity — but the body that carries those things is now something different.

## What You Gain

The first skill of the tree completes the transformation: immunity to mundane [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out), the cessation of aging, and freedom from biological needs. Training tier skills add **+2 Strength**, **Blood Healing** (rapidly restore Health by feeding from a willing or incapacitated creature), **+2 Reflex**, and **Mist Step** (teleportation through any mist or fog within 30 ft.).

The mastery tier unlocks **Dominating Gaze** (compel a target to obey a single command), **Blood Surge** (spend stockpiled blood to dramatically enhance physical capabilities for 1 round), **Mist Form** (full transformation into mist, with immunity to physical damage), and at the pinnacle, **Apex Predator** (which enhances all active abilities and removes feeding constraints during an encounter).

**Strongest abilities:** Blood Healing (fast in-combat recovery), Mist Step (combat mobility), Dominating Gaze (social/combat compulsion), Blood Surge (burst power), Mist Form (temporary invulnerability to physical damage).

## What It Costs

**The hunger.** A vampire must feed (consume blood from a living creature) at least once per session or face escalating consequences. The Narrator tracks hunger privately and introduces it as narrative pressure. Vampires who feed regularly experience no mechanical penalty; those who are denied feeding over multiple sessions will find their capabilities degraded.

**Rest does not restore Health.** Blood is the only restorative. A vampire who cannot feed cannot recover from wounds.

**Silver wounds** suppress Blood Healing until the silver is physically removed. A vampire cannot use Blood Healing to close a silver wound while any silver remains in it — the metal must be extracted before feeding will heal that injury. **Silver weapons also deal 1 additional damage die** against vampires.

Sunlight does not destroy vampires outright but suppresses their abilities and causes escalating pain over prolonged exposure — the Narrator determines severity by circumstance.
