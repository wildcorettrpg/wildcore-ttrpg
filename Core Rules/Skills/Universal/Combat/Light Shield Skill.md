# Light Shield Skill

**Branch:** Combat  
**Tree:** Universal  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Description

Light shields — wooden shields, steel shields, and bucklers — are carried in the off-hand and provide a bonus to Block Opposition Checks rather than passive mitigation. Where heavy shields absorb blows, light shields redirect them: the wielder meets incoming attacks with countermovement, turning them aside rather than bracing for impact. A buckler is the smallest variant — strapped to the forearm rather than gripped, it leaves the hand free for a weapon or item. Training with a light shield refines this deflection instinct, turning a reactive flinch into precise intercept.

## Notes

1. An untrained character wielding a light shield uses the Benefit (untrained) value from the [shield table](../../../Equipment/Armor.md#shields).
2. Light Shield I grants access to the Benefit (trained) value.
3. Light shields require the off-hand to grip. A buckler is the exception — it straps to the forearm, allowing the same arm to carry a one-handed weapon or item.
4. The Block bonus applies only when actively blocking. A character who is [Incapacitated](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#incapacitated), Restrained, or otherwise unable to use the shield loses this bonus.

## Skills

### Light Shield I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Effect:** Trained light shield use. Use the Benefit (trained) column from the [shield table](../../../Equipment/Armor.md#shields) instead of Benefit (untrained).

### Light Shield II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Light Shield I  
**Effect:** +1 to Block Opposition Checks when wielding a light shield (total +1 from this tree).

### Light Shield III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Light Shield II  
**Effect:** +1 to Block Opposition Checks when wielding a light shield (total +2 from this tree).

### Light Shield IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Light Shield III  
**Effect:** **Interpose.** Once per encounter, when an attack targets an adjacent ally, you may use your Reaction to Block on their behalf. Your Block Opposition Check replaces the ally's defense roll for that attack. If your check beats the attack, the blow is deflected. If your check fails, the ally takes the hit normally.

### Light Shield V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Light Shield IV  
**Effect:** **Shield Bash.** You may make a Shield Bash as a melee attack (1 AP). Roll a standard attack Opposition Check (Melee Attack dice + Strength). On a hit, the bash deals 1d4 damage plus the attacker's flat Strength bonus (minimum 1), and the target is [Hindered](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#hindered) until the start of your next turn. The Strength attribute damage bonus (1 bonus die per 3 points of Strength, per [Damage](../../../Encounters/Damage.md) §3) applies normally.
