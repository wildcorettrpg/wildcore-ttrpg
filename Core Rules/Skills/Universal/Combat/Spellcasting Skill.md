# Spellcasting Skill

**Branch:** Combat  
**Tree:** Universal  
**Prerequisites:** A lineage, skill, or campaign setting rule that grants access to magical abilities (see your campaign setting's lineage trees and magic system)

## Description

Spellcasting represents a character's mastery of magic — channeling supernatural power to alter reality and, when necessary, harm a target. The specific tradition (glyphweaving, wavebinding, or whatever form magic takes in a given campaign setting) is defined by that setting's magic system. Skills in this tree improve Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks and spell damage, and raise how much AP a caster can commit to a single cast. At Mastery, the tree splits into two independent branches — Combat, which sharpens raw magical offense, and Overclocking, which builds resilience against the dangers of pushing a spell past its natural cost. Spellcasting is only available to characters with a lineage, skill, or campaign setting rule that grants access to magical abilities.

For the full rules on how magic works — Potency and Charging, the Predation Clock, spell attacks and defense, Overclocking, and Multi-Round Casting — see [Magic](../../../Magic/Magic.md).

## Notes

1. Casting Time for the largest spells is calibrated so that a spell costing roughly 9 AP plays the role a 9th-level spell would have under the d20 System Reference Document's Open Game Content spell-level scale, which Velocity no longer uses directly — see the Legal & Attribution page on the WildCore website for the full license notice.
2. **Survival is part of the craft.** At Spellcasting V, the character has trained deeply enough that pushing their body while channeling — sustaining Concentration, absorbing a hit mid-cast, fighting through injury while spellcasting — no longer knocks them out as easily as it would an untrained person. The +1d10 to Consciousness Checks reflects that conditioning, not a supernatural effect.
3. See the campaign setting for available spells and any setting-specific flavor on how Overclocking's toll manifests (e.g., Anar's Magesea/wavebinding backlash).
4. Mastery splits into two independent branches at Spellcasting V. A character may pursue Combat, Overclocking, both, or neither — neither branch is a prerequisite for the other.

## Training Tier

### Spellcasting I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** A lineage, skill, or campaign setting rule that grants access to magical abilities  
**Effect:** The character may cast spells.

### Spellcasting II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting I  
**Effect:** +2 to Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks.

### Spellcasting III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting II  
**Effect:** +2 spell damage.

### Spellcasting IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting III  
**Effect:** +2 to Concentration Checks.

### Spellcasting V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting IV  
**Effect:** +1d10 to Consciousness Checks (see Note 2).

## Mastery Tier: Combat

### Spellcasting VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting V  
**Effect:** +1 AP (maximum 5 AP per turn).

### Spellcasting VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting VI  
**Effect:** +2 to Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks and spell damage.

### Spellcasting VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting VII  
**Effect:** +1d10 to Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks.

### Spellcasting IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting VIII  
**Effect:** **Baleful Caster:** when the character rolls damage for a spell, they may reroll the lowest damage die — unless that die already shows its maximum value.

### Spellcasting X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting IX  
**Effect:** +1 AP (maximum 5 AP per turn).

## Mastery Tier: Overclocking

### Overclocking I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting V  
**Effect:** +2 to Consciousness Checks triggered by paying an Overclocking Health cost.

### Overclocking II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Overclocking I  
**Effect:** Once per session, an overclocked cast does not affect the Predation Clock.

### Overclocking III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Overclocking II  
**Effect:** Once per encounter, reroll a failed Concentration Check. The higher result stands.

### Overclocking IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Overclocking III  
**Effect:** The character no longer risks Bleeding Out from Health spent overclocking a spell.

### Overclocking V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Overclocking IV  
**Effect:** Once per session, if paying an Overclocking Health cost would reduce the character to 0 Health, they drop to 1 Health instead.
