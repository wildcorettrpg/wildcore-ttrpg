# Spellcasting Skill

**Branch:** Combat  
**Tree:** Universal  
**Prerequisites:** A lineage, skill, or campaign setting rule that grants access to magical abilities (see your campaign setting's lineage trees and magic system)

## Description

Spellcasting represents a character's mastery of magic — channeling supernatural power to alter reality and, when necessary, harm a target. The specific tradition (glyphweaving, wavebinding, or whatever form magic takes in a given campaign setting) is defined by that setting's magic system. Skills in this tree unlock spell levels and improve Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks and spell damage. Spellcasting is only available to characters with a lineage, skill, or campaign setting rule that grants access to magical abilities.

For the full rules on how magic works — Health cost, Predation Clock, spell attacks and defense, overcharging, and creature spellcasting — see [Magic](../../../Encounters/Magic.md).

## Notes

1. The numbered spell level scale (0–9) is adapted from the d20 System Reference Document's Open Game Content — see the Legal & Attribution page on the Velocity website for the full license notice.
2. **Survival is part of the craft.** At Spellcasting V, the character has trained deeply enough that enduring magic's physical toll is no longer incidental — it's learned. A caster at this depth of investment has conditioned their body to fight through damage that would knock an untrained person unconscious. The +1d10 to Consciousness Checks reflects that conditioning, not a supernatural effect.
3. See the campaign setting for available spells and any setting-specific flavor on how the Health cost manifests (e.g., Anar's Magesea/wavebinding toll).

## Training Tier

### Spellcasting I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** A lineage, skill, or campaign setting rule that grants access to magical abilities  
**Effect:** The character may cast Level 0 spells.

### Spellcasting II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting I  
**Effect:** The character may cast 1st level spells. +2 to Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks.

### Spellcasting III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting II  
**Effect:** The character may cast 2nd level spells. +2 spell damage.

### Spellcasting IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting III  
**Effect:** The character may cast 3rd level spells.

### Spellcasting V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting IV  
**Effect:** The character may cast 4th level spells. +1d10 to Consciousness Checks (see Note 2).

## Mastery Tier

### Spellcasting VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting V  
**Effect:** The character may cast 5th level spells. +1 AP (maximum 5 AP per turn).

### Spellcasting VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting VI  
**Effect:** The character may cast 6th level spells. +2 to Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks and spell damage.

### Spellcasting VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting VII  
**Effect:** The character may cast 7th level spells. +1d10 to Spellcasting attack Opposition Checks.

### Spellcasting IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting VIII  
**Effect:** The character may cast 8th level spells. **Baleful Caster:** when the character rolls damage for a spell, they may reroll the lowest damage die — unless that die already shows its maximum value.

### Spellcasting X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Spellcasting IX  
**Effect:** The character may cast 9th level spells. +1 AP (maximum 5 AP per turn).
