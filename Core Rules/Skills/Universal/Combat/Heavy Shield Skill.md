# Heavy Shield Skill

**Branch:** Combat  
**Tree:** Universal  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Description

Heavy shields — wooden shields, steel shields, and tower shields — put something solid between the bearer and whatever is trying to harm them. A wooden shield is the cheapest protection available; a steel shield is heavier and more resilient; a tower shield is a full-length barrier a defender can crouch behind entirely, at the cost of bulk and noise. All three provide flat passive damage mitigation on every hit. Training teaches the bearer to use the shield's coverage intelligently: angling it to catch blows, bracing against heavy strikes, and keeping it positioned without telegraphing movement.

## Notes

1. Wearing a heavy shield without Heavy Shield training uses the Mitigation (untrained) value from the shield table. A tower shield also imposes −2 to Reflex-based Opposition Checks and −2 to Stealth Opposition Checks when untrained.
2. Heavy Shield I grants access to the Mitigation (trained) value and removes the tower shield's −2 Reflex penalty.
3. Each tier from I through IV grants an additional +1 to the shield's mitigation, for a total bonus of +4 at Heavy Shield IV. This bonus is cumulative with the trained mitigation value.
4. Mitigation from a heavy shield stacks with mitigation from worn body armor.

## Skills

### Heavy Shield I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Effect:** 
- Trained heavy shield use. Use the Mitigation (trained) column from the [shield table](../../../Equipment/Armor.md#shields) instead of Mitigation (untrained). 
- Removes the −2 Reflex penalty for untrained tower shield use. 
- +1 to damage mitigation from the equipped heavy shield.

### Heavy Shield II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Heavy Shield I  
**Effect:** 
- Removes the −2 Stealth Opposition Check penalty for untrained tower shield use. 
- +1 to damage mitigation from the equipped heavy shield (total +2 from this tree).

### Heavy Shield III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Heavy Shield II  
**Effect:** 
- +1 to damage mitigation from the equipped heavy shield (total +3 from this tree).

### Heavy Shield IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Heavy Shield III  
**Effect:** 
- +1 to damage mitigation from the equipped heavy shield (total +4 from this tree).

### Heavy Shield V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Heavy Shield IV  
**Effect:** 
- **Shield Bash.** You may make a Shield Bash as a melee attack (1 AP). Roll a standard attack Opposition Check (Melee Attack dice + Strength). On a hit, the bash deals 1d8 damage plus the attacker's flat Strength bonus (minimum 1), and the target is knocked Prone. The Strength attribute damage bonus (1 bonus die per 3 points of Strength, per [Damage](../../../Encounters/Damage.md) §3) applies normally.
