# Social Skill

**Branch:** Attributes  
**Tree:** Universal  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Description

Social represents a character's capacity for social influence — persuasion, intimidation, negotiation, and contests of will or empathy with others. It is the flat bonus on all Social Opposition Checks. A character with high Social commands attention without demanding it; their words land harder, their presence fills a room, and others find them difficult to dismiss or ignore. At mastery, they can hold the attention of a crowd, define the tone of a negotiation before a word is exchanged, and leave impressions that outlast the scene.
