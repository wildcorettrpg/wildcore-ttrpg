# Healing Skill

**Branch:** Survival  
**Tree:** Universal  
**Prerequisites:** None

## Description

Healing is the trained ability to keep people alive under adverse conditions. At its foundation it is improvised — applied pressure, field splinting, surgery with whatever is on hand. A character who invests deeply enough unlocks proper medical equipment: first the Healer's Bag, then the Master Healer's Satchel. Along the way they accumulate a toolkit of techniques that remain useful long after a kit runs empty.

## Notes

1. **Treatment cost** — Each treatment costs 1 AP. Some improvised techniques require additional time outside combat, noted in the tier description.
2. **Healer's Bag** — 5 slots holding up to 5 identical Tiny or Small healing supplies per slot. Using an item removes it from the bag. Unlocked at Healing V. See [Gear](../../../Equipment/Gear.md) §Healer's Supplies.
3. **Master Healer's Satchel** — 10 slots, same per-slot limit. Unlocked at Healing VII. A character with a Satchel may also use a Healer's Bag if preferred.
4. **Heroic Measures** — When treating an injured character with a Healer's Bag or Master Healer's Satchel, the healer may declare Heroic Measures instead of a standard treatment. The Opposition Check difficulty is a Narrator-assigned value based on the severity of the patient's injuries (see the [Opposition Check Difficulty Table](../../../Tables/Opposition%20Check%20Difficulty%20Table.md)), plus 4. On success: the patient is Stabilized, regains consciousness, and recovers Health (Healer's Bag: 1d6 + Willpower; Master Healer's Satchel: 2d6 + Willpower). Costs 1 AP and consumes one item from the bag. Unlocked at Healing VI.

## Training Tier

### Healing I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** None  
**Effect:** +1 to Healing Opposition Checks. The character can apply improvised pressure or a tourniquet to halt [Bleeding Out](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out). The patient remains at their current Health but no longer loses Health from that stack of bleeding. Requires no equipment beyond available cloth or cordage. Can be performed in or out of combat (1 AP). A Healing Opposition Check is required (see [Stabilization](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Stabilization.md).)

### Healing II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing I  
**Effect:** +1 to Healing Opposition Checks. The character can use improvised bandaging to restore 1d4 Health outside combat. Requires scavenged or purchased materials; takes 10 minutes. The character can perform Forced Awakening — see [Stabilization](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Stabilization.md) §Forced Awakening.

### Healing III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing II  
**Effect:** The character can manage pain and minor conditions using herbs, alcohol, or improvised analgesics. Removes the [Hindered](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#hindered) condition and other pain-based conditions from a patient outside combat. Takes one scene.

### Healing IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing III  
**Effect:** The character can perform minor surgery outside combat — sutures, wound closure, tooth extractions, and splinting. Surgery closes wounds against future bleed risk and removes structural conditions. After surgery the patient makes a Fortitude Opposition Check against a difficulty set by the Narrator based on wound severity; on success the patient recovers 1d4 Health. Requires improvised tools (needle, thread, alcohol for sterilization).

### Healing V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing IV  
**Effect:** +1 to Healing Opposition Checks. The character can carry and use a Healer's Bag, applying its contents to treat wounds and conditions in or out of combat (1 AP per treatment, one item consumed per treatment). The character may also treat a [Poisoned](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#poisoned) character: spend 1 AP and consume one item from the bag to halve the Fortitude check difficulty for the remainder of the scene. See [Poisons](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Poisons.md) §Severity Tiers.

## Mastery Tier

### Healing VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing V  
**Effect:** +1 to Healing Opposition Checks. The character may attempt Heroic Measures when treating with a Healer's Bag — see Notes §4.

### Healing VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing VI  
**Effect:** The character can carry and use a Master Healer's Satchel. Heroic Measures performed with a Master Healer's Satchel restore 2d6 + Willpower Health on success instead of 1d6 + Willpower.

### Healing VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing VII  
**Effect:** +1d10 to Healing Opposition Checks. The character may perform Heroic Measures as a Reaction (1 AP) the moment an ally falls [Unconscious](../../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious), without waiting for their turn.

### Healing IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing VIII  
**Effect:** The character can attempt to revive a character who has died within the current scene, provided the body is intact. Requires a Master Healer's Satchel. The Narrator sets the difficulty based on how recently death occurred and the severity of the injuries; failure does not worsen the body's condition.

### Healing X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Healing IX  
**Effect:** +1d10 to Healing Opposition Checks. Heroic Measures no longer incurs the +4 difficulty penalty — the check is made at the base Narrator-assigned difficulty without the +4 addition.
