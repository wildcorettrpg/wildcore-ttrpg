# Lineages

A **lineage** is a supernatural transformation, curse, blessing, pact, or awakening a character acquires through play. It is distinct from a character's **race** — their birth ancestry. A character does not choose a lineage at creation; they enter one when the story calls for it.

Each lineage has its own skill tree. A character who has been turned vampire, bitten by a werewolf, bound to an angel, or corrupted by a demon gains access to that lineage's tree and may spend Skill Points to develop it. Lineage skills are purchased the same way as any other skill — they are not granted automatically with the transformation.

A character may hold more than one lineage simultaneously. Skills from different lineage trees sit on the character sheet alongside each other without interaction. Any narrative tension between a character's lineages — the hunger of the vampire against the mercy of an angelic bond, say — is story material, not a mechanical subsystem.

## Core Lineages

The following lineages are part of the Core Rules and are available in any campaign setting unless a setting explicitly excludes them. Campaign settings may publish their own version of any lineage skill tree in place of the core version.

## Acquiring a Lineage

A lineage is always acquired narratively — it cannot be purchased with Skill Points at character creation. The Narrator and player determine together what the transformation means for the character and what triggered it. Common triggers:

- **Angel:** chosen as an avatar or instrument; marked by divine contact; soul-bound through an act of sacrifice
- **Demon:** forged a pact for power; marked by infernal contact; bound through ritual corruption or a bargain struck in desperation
- **Fae-Touched:** consumed fae food or drink; rescued by a faerie who left a mark; crossed into the fae wild and returned changed; struck a bargain with a faerie who paid with a piece of themselves
- **Vampire:** drained to near-death by a vampire and given the choice to feed; cursed blood; a pact sealed in death
- **Werewolf:** bitten during a full moon attack; cursed by a lycanthrope's blood ritual; born to a werewolf parent with a dormant strain that activates under stress

Once acquired, the lineage's skill tree becomes available immediately. The character begins at tier I and purchases skills with SP as normal.

## Lineage and Race

A character's race does not change when they acquire a lineage. A human turned vampire is still human — still shaped by human culture, human relationships, and human memory. The transformation adds to what they are; it does not replace it. A werewolf who was a dwarven blacksmith is still a dwarven blacksmith — now with claws.

This is intentional. WildCore does not use lineages to reassign a character's identity. They are something that happened to the character. What the character does with it belongs to play, not to the rules.
