# Fae-Touched Lineage

**Type:** Lineage (acquired through play)  
**Prerequisite:** Narratively acquired — see [Lineages](Lineages.md#acquiring-a-lineage)

## Description

The Fae-Touched lineage represents a character who has been marked by the fae world — not born into it like a Faerie, but claimed by it through contact, bargain, consumption of fae food or drink, rescue by a faerie who left something behind, or simply crossing into the fae wild and returning changed. The mark is real and permanent. Something in the character now resonates with fae nature, and that resonance deepens as they develop it.

Fae-Touched characters are not fae. They retain their race's identity and nature. What they carry is a layer of fae attunement — a sensitivity to deception, a capacity for glamour, and a growing connection to the wild places and the old bargains that govern fae behavior. As that attunement deepens, they find iron increasingly uncomfortable: not the acute pain it causes a true faerie, but a dull persistent wrongness that sharpens with proximity.

Fae-Touched characters can lie. Unlike true faeries, they are not metaphysically bound to truth. What they cannot do is remain unaware of lies — the mark makes deception visible to them in a way it wasn't before.

## Training Tier

### Fae-Touched I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae marking (narrative)  
**Effect:** The character is marked. They develop passive truth-sense: within 30 ft. of a speaker, they always know when something said is a deliberate lie — not the truth of what the speaker believes, but whether the speaker knows they are deceiving. This cannot be suppressed. They also see through fae glamours automatically — illusions created by faeries or fae magic are transparent to them.

### Fae-Touched II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched I  
**Effect:** +2 Social. The fae mark manifests as uncanny presence — a quality of attention and grace that mortals find difficult to look away from.

### Fae-Touched III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched II  
**Effect:** **Lightborne Step.** The character ignores movement penalties from difficult terrain. In natural environments (forests, wild places, open fields), they move as if the ground offers no resistance — floating just above the surface without conscious effort. +4 bonus to Stealth Opposition Checks when using Lightborne Step.

### Fae-Touched IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched III  
**Effect:** **Minor Glamour.** The character may create a sensory illusion affecting one target within 30 ft. (1 AP). The illusion has no physical substance — it can appear as a person, object, or scene, but physical contact immediately reveals it. The glamour lasts until the character dismisses it or moves more than 60 ft. away. Creating the illusion does not require Concentration.

### Fae-Touched V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched IV  
**Effect:** **Veil of Passing.** Once per encounter, the character may spend 1 AP to become unnaturally unnoticeable for 1 round. They are not invisible — they can be seen if someone specifically looks for them — but they do not register to casual observation. Stealth Opposition Checks made this round gain +1d10.

## Mastery Tier

### Fae-Touched VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched V  
**Effect:** +2 Social. The fae mark is now visible to those who know what to look for — a quality of light around the character, eyes that catch reflections at odd angles, a voice that carries slightly too clearly. Fae creatures recognize them as claimed.

### Fae-Touched VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched VI  
**Effect:** **Fae Bargain.** Once per session, the character may offer a binding exchange (1 AP). Both parties state their terms aloud; if both agree freely, the terms become magically binding. Neither party can voluntarily break the terms without being [Incapacitated](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#incapacitated) each time they attempt to do so — the compulsion prevents the action rather than punishing it afterward. The binding lasts until the terms are fulfilled or both parties mutually release it. Agreement cannot be compelled; both must accept freely.

### Fae-Touched VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched VII  
**Effect:** **Greater Glamour.** Minor Glamour (Fae-Touched IV) now affects all creatures within 30 ft. simultaneously and engages all senses — sight, sound, smell, even touch up to the point of impact. Physical substance remains absent; a glamoured wall stops the eye but not the body. Duration extends to 1 hour or until dismissed.

### Fae-Touched IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched VIII  
**Effect:** **Ethereal Form.** Once per session, the character may assume an ethereal state (1 AP), lasting up to 1 hour or until dismissed (1 AP). While ethereal, the character cannot physically interact with objects or creatures — they pass through them without resistance. Normal (non-magical) weapons cannot harm them. Magical weapons, spells, and mind-affecting abilities affect them normally. While ethereal, the character heals at 2 Health per hour of rest and doubles Health restored by potions and Healing skills. They also gain +2 on Opposition Checks to resist magical effects. A faint silver or golden luminescence emanates from the character while in this state — they cannot be [Stealthy](../../Skills/Universal/Survival/Stealth%20Skill.md) while ethereal.

### Fae-Touched X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Fae-Touched IX  
**Effect:** **Fae Ascendant.** The character is unmistakably claimed. Fae creatures and those with supernatural awareness recognize them on sight. Fae beings who wish to attack them directly must succeed on a Willpower Opposition Check (difficulty 14) — the old laws of the fae world create a compulsion against harming the claimed. Veil of Passing (Fae-Touched V) may be used twice per encounter. Fae Bargain (Fae-Touched VII) may be used twice per session.

## Notes

1. **Iron and steel weapons** deal 1 additional damage die against Fae-Touched characters. This mirrors the Touch of Iron flaw carried by the Faerie race — the fae bond makes the character's body respond to iron the same way a Faerie's does.
2. **Truth-sense limitations.** The passive truth-sense of Fae-Touched I detects deliberate deception — a speaker who genuinely believes a false thing registers as truthful. The sense cannot be suppressed, but it also cannot be weaponized against the character: knowing when someone is lying does not compel the character to reveal it.
3. **Fae Bargain and willing agreement.** The binding requires both parties to agree freely. Coercion, magical compulsion, or deception that materially misrepresents the terms voids the binding. The Narrator determines what constitutes free agreement in contested cases.
4. **Relationship to the Faerie race.** A Faerie character who acquires the Fae-Touched lineage is deepening a connection they were born with. The Narrator should consider what that means in their setting — whether a Faerie becoming Fae-Touched represents a deeper awakening, a second claiming, or something unprecedented.
