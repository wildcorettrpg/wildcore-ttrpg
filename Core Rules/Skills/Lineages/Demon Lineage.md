# Demon Lineage

**Type:** Lineage (acquired through play)  
**Prerequisite:** Narratively acquired — see [Lineages](Lineages.md#acquiring-a-lineage)

## Description

The Demon lineage represents infernal corruption — a connection to a demonic power that has marked, bound, or claimed the character. This is not always a voluntary transaction. Characters may enter a demonic lineage through a desperate pact, through exposure to infernal energy, or because something dark took an interest in them and made its mark without asking.

The gifts the Demon lineage provides are real. So is what they cost. The infernal power behind the bond is not a benefactor — it has its own agenda, and the character's growing capability is also growing leverage. The bond does not demand obedience in any mechanical sense; it creates pressure, temptation, and narrative complication that the Narrator introduces when dramatically appropriate.

The skill tree represents the character's growing command over infernal energy — learning to use it deliberately rather than be used by it, and eventually wielding it with the precision of a weapon.

## Training Tier

### Demon I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Infernal bond (narrative)  
**Effect:** The character is marked. Their eyes carry a faint unnatural quality (color shift, faint glow, unusual pupil shape — the player and Narrator define this). They are immune to mundane fear effects — only magical fear ([Frightened](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#frightened) applied by an ability or spell) affects them. They can speak and understand any language, though this does not grant literacy.

### Demon II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon I  
**Effect:** **Infernal Resilience.** The character gains 2 points of damage mitigation against fire damage. Their body runs hot — they are comfortable in extreme heat that would [Incapacitate](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#incapacitated) a normal person.

### Demon III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon II  
**Effect:** +2 Willpower. The infernal bond sharpens the character's will into something dangerous.

### Demon IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon III  
**Effect:** **Shadowstep.** Once per encounter, the character may spend 1 AP to teleport from one shadow to another within 30 ft. The origin and destination must both be in dim light or darkness. The character arrives in the destination shadow without passing through the intervening space.

### Demon V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon IV  
**Effect:** **Hellfire Touch.** The character's melee attacks may deal fire damage instead of their normal damage type (declared before rolling). Once per scene, they may channel hellfire into a touch attack (1 AP, Melee Attack Opposition Check): on a hit, 2d6 fire damage, unmitigated by armor.

## Mastery Tier

### Demon VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon V  
**Effect:** +2 Willpower. The character's demonic presence is palpable — those who interact with them for more than a few minutes sense something unsettling, even if they cannot name it. Intimidation Opposition Checks against mortals gain +1d10.

### Demon VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon VI  
**Effect:** **Infernal Command.** Once per scene, the character may issue a compulsion to a single target within 30 ft. (1 AP, Social Opposition Check vs. the target's Willpower). On success, the target is [Incapacitated](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#incapacitated) for 1 round and must use their next action to follow a single command that does not directly harm them (retreat, be silent, hand over an object). The effect is not detected as magical by mundane means.

### Demon VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon VII  
**Effect:** **Wings of Ash.** The character manifests great dark wings. They may fly at their base movement rate (1 AP = full base movement). The wings shed faint ash or embers when extended — this can be suppressed with concentration (1 AP to suppress, lasts until the character takes an action or Reaction).

### Demon IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon VIII  
**Effect:** **Soul Sight.** The character can perceive the moral weight of what others have done — not a full record of deeds, but an impression of what a person carries. This is not a mechanical truth-detection ability; it is narrative texture the Narrator uses to convey information. The character also cannot be deceived by illusions while this sight is active (passive, always on).

### Demon X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Demon IX  
**Effect:** **Infernal Ascendant.** Hellfire Touch (Demon V) no longer has a once-per-scene limit. Infernal Command (Demon VII) may now be used twice per scene. Shadowstep (Demon IV) may now be used twice per encounter, and its range extends to 60 ft. The character's infernal mitigation (Demon II) increases to 4 points against fire damage and extends to cover all elemental damage (cold, lightning, acid) at 2 points. The bond is now visible to anyone with supernatural sight — the character carries the mark openly, whether they wish to or not.

## Notes

1. **Pact terms** are a story element, not a mechanic. If the infernal power wants something specific, that is a narrative obligation the Narrator develops. The rules do not define what demons want — that belongs to the campaign setting.
2. **Moral alignment** is not attached to the Demon lineage. A character with a demonic bond is not evil by default; they are carrying something that has its own interests and agenda. What they do with that is a story question.
3. **Interactions with angelic lineages:** a character holding both an Angel and Demon lineage is under extraordinary pressure from two directions. The rules impose no mechanical resolution; the tension is the story.
4. **Fire immunity vs. mitigation:** the Demon lineage grants mitigation, not immunity. A character at Demon II takes 2 less fire damage per hit. At Demon X, 4 less per hit. Neither makes them invulnerable — a sufficiently powerful fire source will still burn them.
