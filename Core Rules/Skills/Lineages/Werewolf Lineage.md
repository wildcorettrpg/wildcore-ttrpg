# Werewolf Lineage

**Type:** Lineage (acquired through play)  
**Prerequisite:** Narratively acquired — see [Lineages](Lineages.md#acquiring-a-lineage)

## Description

Lycanthropy is a curse that runs in the blood — a predatory spirit grafted onto the soul, manifesting as the ability to shift into a hybrid beast-human form or a full wolf form. A werewolf character does not lose themselves to the beast; they learn to coexist with it, channel it, and eventually command it.

Werewolves heal rapidly. They are immune to most diseases and poisons. Silver and wolfsbane both suppress their accelerated healing — wounds caused by silver weapons, or affecting a character who has been exposed to wolfsbane, close at the standard rate rather than the lycanthropic rate. The full moon creates pressure on the beast — not an automatic transformation at low tiers, but a pull that strengthens as the moon rises, and a window in which the beast is closest to the surface.

The skill tree represents the character's growing ability to control their lycanthropy: shifting on demand, holding form under pressure, and developing the beast's capabilities without losing themselves to it.

## The Beast

The werewolf's animal instincts are a permanent presence — not a mechanical condition, but a narrative one. When provoked, cornered, or exposed to silver, the beast asserts itself. The Narrator may call for the character to resist an instinctive reaction (flee, attack, protect the pack). Resistance is a Willpower Opposition Check against a Narrator-assigned difficulty. Failure does not mean the character loses control — it means they act on instinct before they can think. This is story texture, not a separate subsystem.

## Training Tier

### Werewolf I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Lycanthropic transformation (narrative)  
**Effect:** The character gains the lycanthropic strain. They heal at 2 Health per hour of rest (instead of the normal 1 per 4 hours). They are immune to mundane diseases and poisons (magical diseases and poisons affect them normally). Silver wounds and wolfsbane exposure suppress accelerated healing — affected wounds close at the standard rate rather than the lycanthropic rate.

### Werewolf II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf I  
**Effect:** **Partial Shift.** The character may shift specific features — claws, teeth, heightened senses — without entering full beast form (1 AP). Claws deal 1d6 + Strength damage. Bite deals 1d8 + Strength damage. These natural weapons require no proficiency and impose no untrained penalty.

### Werewolf III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf II  
**Effect:** +2 Strength. The lycanthropic body carries more raw physical power even in human form.

### Werewolf IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf III  
**Effect:** **Keen Senses.** +2 on Perception Opposition Checks. The character can track by scent with the same effectiveness as sight — [Blind](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#blind) does not impair their ability to locate targets within 30 ft.

### Werewolf V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf IV  
**Effect:** **Full Shift.** The character may enter full wolf or hybrid form (2 AP). In hybrid form: +2 Strength, +2 Reflex, natural weapons deal 1d10 + Strength (claws) and 1d12 + Strength (bite). In wolf form: +2 Reflex, movement rate doubles, natural weapons deal 1d8 + Strength. Shifting back to human form costs 1 AP. The character retains full mental faculties in both forms.

## Mastery Tier

### Werewolf VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf V  
**Effect:** +2 Strength. At this tier, the lycanthrope's strength in hybrid form is formidable — they can throw a person like a sack, shatter wooden doors, and wrestle large animals to the ground.

### Werewolf VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf VI  
**Effect:** **Lycan Healing.** Once per scene, as a free action, the character may spend 5 Health to immediately heal 3d6 Health. This represents the lycanthropic regeneration surging in response to injury. Cannot be used while [Unconscious](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#unconscious).

### Werewolf VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf VII  
**Effect:** **Pack Bond.** The character may designate up to four allies as pack. Pack members gain +1 on all Opposition Checks while within 30 ft. of the werewolf and in the same encounter. The werewolf always knows the direction and approximate distance to any pack member within 1 mile.

### Werewolf IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf VIII  
**Effect:** **Fearsome Presence.** Once per encounter, when the character enters hybrid or full shift form (or is already in one), they may spend 1 AP to force all enemies within 30 ft. to make a Willpower Opposition Check (difficulty 14). Those who fail are [Frightened](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#frightened) for 2 rounds.

### Werewolf X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Werewolf IX  
**Effect:** **Apex Wolf.** The character shifts form in 1 AP (down from 2 AP). Full shift no longer requires concentration to maintain — the character may use all skills and abilities normally while shifted. Lycan Healing (Werewolf VII) no longer has a once-per-scene limit. The character is immune to the full moon's instinctive pull — they shift and hold form entirely at will, at any time.

## Notes

1. **Full moon pressure** applies to characters at Werewolf I–IV who have not yet mastered their shift. The Narrator may call for Willpower checks during a full moon to avoid partial involuntary shifting or acting on the beast's instincts. At Werewolf V and above, the character can shift at will and the moon is no longer a source of pressure unless a specific ability or setting rule says otherwise.
2. **Infecting others** via bite is a narrative event, not an automatic mechanical result. The Narrator determines whether a bite wound could transmit lycanthropy based on story context and setting lore.
3. **Silver weapons** deal 1 additional damage die against werewolves. Silver also suppresses accelerated healing: a werewolf cannot benefit from their passive healing (Werewolf I) or Lycan Healing (Werewolf VII) to close a silver wound while any silver remains in the wound. Removing silver from a wound is a medical action (Narrator-assigned difficulty based on depth and severity).
4. **Wolfsbane** (aconite) suppresses all wound healing for the duration of the poisoning — both passive regeneration and Lycan Healing are suspended. See [Poisons](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Poisons.md#wolfsbane).
