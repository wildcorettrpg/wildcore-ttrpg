# Angel Lineage

**Type:** Lineage (acquired through play)  
**Prerequisite:** Narratively acquired — see [Lineages](Lineages.md#acquiring-a-lineage)

## Description

The Angel lineage represents a divine bond — a connection to a celestial power that has chosen, marked, or claimed the character. The character does not become an angel; they become something touched by one. What that bond looks like varies: an avatar chosen for a specific purpose, a soul marked in a moment of extraordinary sacrifice, a mortal bound through a pact with a celestial intermediary.

The bond imposes obligations alongside its gifts. The celestial power that granted the connection has its own interests, and characters with an Angel lineage find that certain actions — betrayal, cruelty, deliberate harm to the innocent — create friction with the bond that manifests as suppressed abilities or escalating pressure. This is not a mechanical penalty system; it is narrative texture the Narrator introduces when dramatically appropriate.

The skill tree represents growing attunement to the bond — learning to channel divine energy, project it outward, and eventually act as a full instrument of celestial will.

## Training Tier

### Angel I

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Divine bond (narrative)  
**Effect:** The character is marked. They emit faint light (controllable, 5 ft. radius), can always tell true north, and cannot be deceived by mundane disguise — they perceive the person beneath the mask. Mundane diseases do not affect them.

### Angel II

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel I  
**Effect:** **Lay on Hands.** Once per scene, the character may touch a living creature and restore 2d6 Health (1 AP). This ability heals; it does not harm undead or demonic creatures. Stabilization via Lay on Hands requires no equipment and succeeds automatically if the character reaches the target in time.

### Angel III

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel II  
**Effect:** +2 Willpower. The divine bond strengthens the character's inner resolve.

### Angel IV

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel III  
**Effect:** **Celestial Ward.** The character gains 2 points of damage mitigation against purely magical damage — effects that normally bypass armor (force, conjured weapons, purely magical abilities). This mitigation does not apply to physical or elemental damage.

### Angel V

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel IV  
**Effect:** **Wings (Partial).** The character manifests partial wings. They can glide from height without taking falling damage and may hover in place for up to 1 round per encounter (no AP cost). They cannot fly freely at this tier.

## Mastery Tier

### Angel VI

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel V  
**Effect:** +2 Willpower. The bond deepens. The character's presence is palpably calm — those within 10 ft. who are [Frightened](../../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#frightened) may immediately reroll their Opposition Check to end the condition.

### Angel VII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel VI  
**Effect:** **Smite.** Once per encounter, the character channels divine energy into a melee or ranged attack (declared before rolling). On a hit, the attack deals +3d6 bonus damage (unmitigated — bypasses armor). The bonus damage does not benefit from Strength or Willpower damage bonuses.

### Angel VIII

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel VII  
**Effect:** **Wings (Full).** The character's wings fully manifest. They may fly at their base movement rate (1 AP = full base movement, same as ground movement). Flight is available at will.

### Angel IX

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel VIII  
**Effect:** **Aura of Resolve.** Allies within 30 ft. of the character gain +1d10 on Opposition Checks to resist fear, compulsion, and domination effects. This aura is always active while the character is conscious.

### Angel X

**Cost:** 1 SP  
**Prerequisite:** Angel IX  
**Effect:** **Divine Instrument.** Lay on Hands (Angel II) now restores 4d6 Health and has no once-per-scene limit. Smite (Angel VII) may be used twice per encounter. The character's light expands to a 30 ft. radius at full intensity and can suppress magical darkness within that range. The celestial power's bond is now manifest — the character's face is recognizable to anyone with divine sight or sufficient supernatural awareness as an instrument of their patron.

## Notes

1. **Obligation and friction** are narrative tools, not mechanical conditions. The Narrator decides when a character's actions create tension with the bond and how that tension manifests — suppressed abilities, pressure from the celestial power, or direct intervention. These moments should serve the story, not punish the player.
2. **The celestial patron** has its own personality, agenda, and communication style. What they want from the character is a story question, not a rules question. The Core Rules do not define the patron's nature — that belongs to the campaign setting.
3. **Interactions with demonic lineages:** a character holding both an Angel and Demon lineage is carrying an extraordinary narrative weight. The rules impose no mechanical conflict between them; the story conflict is the point.
