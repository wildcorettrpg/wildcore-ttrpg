# Health

## Maximum Health and Current Health

**Health** has two values:

- **Maximum Health** — the ceiling a character's current Health cannot exceed. A character's Maximum Health is determined at character creation by their [Health Archetype](#health-archetypes) (20, 25, or 30). Maximum Health does not decrease during play unless a rule explicitly reduces it. It increases only through the [Health Skill](../../Skills/Universal/Attributes/Health%20Skill.md) tree, [Fortitude](../../Skills/Universal/Attributes/Fortitude%20Skill.md) mastery skills (VII and IX each grant +10), or race abilities.
- **Current Health** — a character's current physical condition. It is reduced by damage and increased by healing. Current Health cannot exceed Maximum Health and cannot be reduced below 0.

## Starting Values

A character's starting Maximum Health is determined by their Health Archetype, chosen at character creation: **20** (Frail), **25** (Average), or **30** (Robust). See [Health Archetypes](#health-archetypes) below. All characters begin play with Current Health equal to their Maximum Health — every new character starts at full Health.

### Health Archetypes

At character creation, a player may declare a Health Archetype reflecting their character's natural constitution. This is an optional choice — characters who make no declaration use the standard baseline.

|Archetype|Maximum Health|SP Adjustment|
|---|---|---|
|Frail|20|+1 SP|
|Average|25|—|
|Robust|30|−1 SP|

**Frail** characters begin with 20 Maximum Health and receive 1 additional SP at character creation. A Frail character may not purchase [Health I](../../Skills/Universal/Attributes/Health%20Skill.md#health%20i) at character creation. The entire [Health Skill](../../Skills/Universal/Attributes/Health%20Skill.md) tree is unavailable until they earn their first [Skill Point](Skill%20Points.md) (SP) through play.

**Robust** characters begin with 30 Maximum Health and pay 1 SP from their starting pool to do so.

**Average** characters begin with 25 Maximum Health and require no declaration.

Health Archetypes represent a character's natural constitution — the physical reality they were born with. They have no bearing on what a character may attempt during play, and they do not affect how quickly a character can advance through the Health skill tree once play begins.

## Health Changes

When a character takes damage, reduce their Current Health by the damage amount after mitigation is applied. When a character receives healing, increase their Current Health by the amount healed, to a maximum of their Maximum Health. Health changes are applied immediately.

## Danger Threshold

When a character's Current Health drops to **7 or below** from any source — incoming damage, [Bleeding Out](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Conditions.md#bleeding-out), a fall, or anything else — they must make a **Consciousness Check**. This threshold is fixed for all characters regardless of Maximum Health. See [Consciousness Checks](../Encounters/Consciousness%20Checks.md) for the full procedure.

A character whose Current Health reaches **0** dies instantly — see [Death](../Conditions%20and%20Hazards/Death.md).
