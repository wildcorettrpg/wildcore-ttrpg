# Dying

## Wounded

A character enters the **Wounded** condition when their Health falls to or below their **wounded threshold** — half their maximum Vitality, rounded up. The moment they enter Wounded, they begin **Bleeding Out**, losing 1 Health at the end of every turn. Despite being hurt, a Wounded character can still attack, defend, cast spells, and take other actions without penalty. The Wounded condition ends when their Health is restored above the wounded threshold, or when they die.

## Bleeding Out

A character who is Bleeding Out loses **1 Health** at the end of each of their turns. If this loss drops their Health to or below their **dying threshold**, they enter the Dying condition. If it reaches 0, they die instantly. Bleeding Out is removed only by [Stabilization](Stabilization.md) or healing.

## Dying

A character enters the **Dying** condition when their Health falls to or below their **dying threshold** — one quarter of their maximum Vitality, rounded up. 

A Dying character suffers **−2** on all Opposition Checks and their movement speed is reduced by **5 ft** per AP spent on movement. They can still attack, defend, cast spells, and take other actions — but at reduced effectiveness, and they remain Bleeding Out until stabilized. 

A Dying character must make a Consciousness Check when they enter the Dying condition, when they take damage, or when they lose Health from Bleeding Out. 

The Dying condition ends when their Health is restored above the dying threshold, or when they die.

## Consciousness Checks

A character in the Dying condition must attempt a Consciousness Check when any of the following occur: they enter the Dying condition, they take damage while Dying, or they lose Health from Bleeding Out while Dying. A character who is already Unconscious does not make further Consciousness Checks.

A Consciousness Check is a special check — not a standard Opposition Check. The character rolls their dice pool + **Willpower**, adding dice and flat bonuses from applicable skills. If the result meets or exceeds the damage value that triggered the check, the character remains conscious. If the result falls short, the character becomes **Unconscious**.

An Unconscious Dying character no longer makes Consciousness Checks but continues Bleeding Out. They cannot defend themselves and are **Vulnerable** to attacks.
