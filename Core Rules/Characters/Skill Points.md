# Skill Points

## Skill Point System

Skill Points (SP) represent a character's advancement. They are awarded only by the Narrator — never earned from combat difficulty, repetition, kills, items, abilities, or any other source. SP are spent to purchase skills, and a character's total spent SP determines their [Effective Level](Effective%20Level.md).

## Skill Point Awards

SP are awarded at the end of a session, after play has concluded. Awards are never given mid-session. There are three award types:

- **Attendance award** (optional): 1 SP to every player who attends. The Narrator decides at session zero whether this award will be used for the campaign — it applies to all players equally or not at all.
- **Story milestone award:** 1 SP to every party member when the party reaches a Narrator-defined milestone.
- **Exceptional play award:** 1 SP to any player whose individual moment elevated the story. This is at the Narrator's discretion, not guaranteed every session; multiple players may earn it in the same session.

See Awarding Skill Points for full guidance on when and how to award SP.

## Spending and Retraining

Unspent SP are spent between sessions, outside of play — not during an encounter or in the middle of a scene. A character may use unspent SP to purchase any skill they qualify for.

Retraining allows a character to reclaim a spent SP by spending **2 unspent SP** to remove **1 spent SP**. When a SP is reclaimed, the character immediately loses all benefits and penalties granted by the removed skill. Retraining occurs between sessions and requires Narrator approval.
