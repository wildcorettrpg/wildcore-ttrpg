# Character Creation

Character creation is the one moment in WildCore where the **No Unnecessary Math** principle yields to necessity. The choices made here define who the character is at the start of play — and, through the flaw and milestone systems, who they have the potential to become. Take the time to make them deliberately.

Work through the following steps in order.

---

## Step 1 — Choose Your Race

Select a race from the [Races](../Races/) folder. Your race determines your starting traits, racial flaws, languages, size, and base movement speed. Read the full race document before proceeding — the traits and flaws your race provides will inform several decisions in the steps that follow.

**Multi-race characters:** If you are building a multi-race character, follow the rules in [Multi-Race Characters](../Races/Multi-Race%20Characters.md) before continuing. Multi-race character creation modifies Step 4 (flaws) and expands your access to racial skill trees.

---

## Step 2 — Choose a Health Archetype

Choose one of the three Health Archetypes. This represents your character's natural constitution and affects both your starting Maximum Health and your starting Skill Point pool.

| Archetype | Maximum Health | SP Adjustment |
| :-------- | :------------- | :------------ |
| Frail     | 20             | +1 SP         |
| Average   | 25             | —             |
| Robust    | 30             | −1 SP         |

Frail characters may not purchase Health I at character creation. The full Health skill tree becomes available once they earn their first Skill Point through play.

See [Health](Health.md) for full details.

---

## Step 3 — Assign Attributes

All five attributes begin at **0**. Assign **2 attribute points** across any combination of attributes. No attribute may exceed **3** at character creation.

The five attributes are **Fortitude**, **Reflex**, **Social**, **Strength**, and **Willpower**.

See [Attributes](Attributes.md) for full descriptions and damage modifier rules.

---

## Step 4 — Select Personal Flaws

Select at least one personal flaw from [Flaws](Flaws.md). Personal flaws represent your character's specific habits, fears, compulsions, and failings — the things that make them who they are, not what their blood dictates. They are separate from and in addition to the racial flaws granted by your race.

**Skill Point bonus:** For each personal flaw selected beyond the first, gain 1 bonus Skill Point. This bonus is capped at three additional flaws — a maximum of +3 SP for a character entering play with four personal flaws. Additional flaws beyond four may be taken voluntarily but grant no further Skill Points.

**Removing flaws:** Personal flaws cannot be removed through Skill Point investment. They are removed only through narrative milestones — events in play that directly confront the flaw and give the character cause to change. When a flaw is removed this way, the character gains 1 Skill Point.

---

## Step 5 — Spend Skill Points

Calculate your starting Skill Point pool, then spend it on skills and attribute nodes.

### Starting Skill Point Pool

| Source | SP |
| :----- | :- |
| Base (all characters) | 10 |
| Human Potential (Humans only) | +2 |
| Health Archetype: Frail | +1 |
| Health Archetype: Robust | −1 |
| Personal flaws (2nd flaw) | +1 |
| Personal flaws (3rd flaw) | +1 |
| Personal flaws (4th flaw) | +1 |

Skill Points are spent to purchase skills. Skills are organized into trees — each tree represents a discipline, and skills within a tree must generally be purchased in order. A character's total spent Skill Points equals their [Effective Level](Effective%20Level.md).

Unspent Skill Points carry into play and may be spent between sessions.

See [Skill Points](Skill%20Points.md) for full rules on spending and retraining.

---

## Step 6 — Purchase Starting Equipment

Every character begins play with a universal starting kit provided at no cost. In addition, each character receives **100 gp** to spend on weapons, armor, and additional gear before the first session.

See [Starting Equipment](Starting%20Equipment.md) for the full starting kit contents, purchasing guidelines, and notes on the 100 gp budget.

---

## Summary Checklist

- [ ] Race selected; traits, racial flaws, languages, size, and movement recorded
- [ ] Health Archetype chosen; Maximum Health and SP adjustment noted
- [ ] 2 attribute points assigned; no attribute above 3
- [ ] At least one personal flaw selected; bonus SP from additional flaws calculated
- [ ] Starting Skill Point pool calculated and spent
- [ ] Starting equipment recorded; 100 gp spent
