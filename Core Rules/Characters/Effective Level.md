# Effective Level

## Definition

Effective Level (EL) is a numerical measure of total character capability. It equals the number of Skill Points a character has spent on skills and attribute nodes. Unspent Skill Points do not count. Effective Level is used for character creation, NPC creation, enemy creation, and as the basis for group effective level.

## Calculating Effective Level

A character's Effective Level is simply their total spent Skill Points. There is no formula beyond that — buy a skill, your EL goes up by the cost of that skill. Characters with a higher Effective Level have invested more Skill Points and possess greater breadth or depth of abilities.

## Effective Level and NPCs

NPCs use the same Effective Level calculation as player characters. To create an NPC, assign a number of Skill Points equal to the desired Effective Level, purchase skills and attribute nodes as appropriate, and record the resulting abilities and modifiers. For monsters ported from d20-based bestiaries (Challenge Rating stat blocks), see Porting d20 Monsters for converting CR directly to a Skill Point budget.

## Base and Group Effective Level

**Base Effective Level** is the average Effective Level of all members of a group, rounded up to the nearest whole number. It represents the group's baseline capability before group size is factored in.

**Group Effective Level** accounts for both the Base Effective Level of the group and the number of members. To find it, determine the group size, determine the Base Effective Level, then look up the result in the [Party Effective Level Calculation table](../Tables/Party%20Effective%20Level%20Calculation.md). No math is required at the table — it's a direct lookup by group size and base EL.
