# Recovery

## Recovery Dice

1. Rest and recuperation uses fixed values: 1 Health per four hours of nonstrenuous activity, 1d4 for a light rest, 2d4 for a long rest.
2. A lineage may specify different recovery values, replacing these defaults.

## Rest and Recuperation

1. A character recovers 1 Health for every four hours of nonstrenuous activity.
2. A light rest of less than six hours restores 1d4 Health.
3. A long rest of six or more hours restores 2d4 Health.
4. Rest and recuperation cannot increase a character’s Health above their Vitality.
5. Rest and recuperation is halted during combat.
6. Rest and recuperation is halted during periods of strenuous activity.
7. Rest and recuperation is halted while the character is dying.

## Healing

1. A character with at least Healing I can provide treatment to recover Health or remove conditions. The form of treatment and equipment required depend on the healer’s Healing tier — see [Healing](../Skills/Universal/Survival/Healing.md) for the full progression.
2. At Healing V, the healer may apply items from a Healer’s Bag (1 AP per treatment, one item consumed). At Healing VII, they may instead use a Master Healer’s Satchel. Item effects are defined in [Gear](../Equipment/Gear.md) §Healer’s Supplies.
3. Heroic Measures (Healing VI+) is a special intervention for Wounded or Dying characters. On a successful Opposition Check against a Narrator-assigned difficulty (based on injury severity) plus 4, the patient is Stabilized, regains consciousness, and recovers 1d6 + Willpower Health (Healer’s Bag) or 2d6 + Willpower (Master Healer’s Satchel). See [Healing](../Skills/Universal/Survival/Healing.md) §Notes.
4. Healing potions restore Health immediately on use — no check required. See [Gear](../Equipment/Gear.md) §Healing Potions for available grades and effects.

## Unconsciousness

1. A stabilized character who is unconscious remains unconscious until a healer removes the Unconscious condition from them (see [Stabilization](Stabilization.md) — Effects of Stabilization), or until forcibly awakened (see [Stabilization](Stabilization.md) — Forced Awakening).
2. A forcibly awakened character retains all conditions present at the time of awakening except Unconscious. See [Stabilization](Stabilization.md) — Forced Awakening.
3. A character who regains consciousness naturally may act normally unless affected by other conditions.

## Restrictions on Recovery

1. A character cannot recover Health through rest while Dying.
2. A character cannot recover Health while dead.
3. A character cannot recover Health through more than one method in the same recovery action — only one of rest and recuperation or healing may apply at a time.
4. A character cannot exceed their Vitality through recovery.
